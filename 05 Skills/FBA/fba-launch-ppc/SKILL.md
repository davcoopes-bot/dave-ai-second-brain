# FBA Launch & PPC Playbook

Built for Dave and Josh. First product launch. $60k budget — protect it. This is a structured, systematic launch, not spray-and-pray PPC.

---

## Phase 1 — Pre-Launch Checklist

Do not spend a dollar on ads until every item below is done. Running PPC to a half-built listing is burning money.

**Listing**
- [ ] Title: primary keyword in first 5 words, readable, under 200 characters
- [ ] Bullets: 5 bullets, each leading with a benefit, secondary keywords woven in naturally
- [ ] Backend keywords: 249 bytes used, no brand names, no repetition of title keywords
- [ ] Description / A+ Content: written and live (A+ requires Brand Registry)
- [ ] Images: 7 images minimum — hero, lifestyle, infographic, size/scale, packaging, use case, competitor comparison
- [ ] Hero image: white background, product fills 85%+ of frame, no text overlays

**Brand & Account**
- [ ] Brand Registry: applied for (takes 2-4 weeks — start this on day 1, not day 30)
- [ ] Vine enrolment: queued immediately after Brand Registry clears

**Inventory**
- [ ] Shipment created and checked in to FBA
- [ ] Status shows as "Active" in Seller Central
- [ ] At least 150-200 units on hand for launch (running out during launch destroys ranking)

**Financials**
- [ ] COGS calculated per unit
- [ ] FBA fees calculated (use FBA Revenue Calculator)
- [ ] Break-even ACOS calculated: (Price - COGS - FBA fees) / Price x 100
- [ ] Launch price set (see Phase 2)
- [ ] Daily PPC budget cap agreed: do not exceed $120/day total across all campaigns in weeks 1-4
- [ ] Weekly PPC review scheduled: every Monday, 20 minutes

## Phase 2 — Launch Pricing Strategy

**Launch price target: 10-15% below the average page 1 price for your main keyword.**

Why this works: Amazon's A9 algorithm weights recent sales velocity heavily. A lower price drives more units sold, which improves conversion rate, which improves organic rank, which compounds. You are buying rank, not profit, in weeks 1-6.

**How to find the right price:**
1. Search your primary keyword on Amazon
2. Record the price of the top 10 organic results (exclude sponsored)
3. Average them — this is your benchmark
4. Set your launch price at 10-15% below that number

**When to raise price:**
- You have 20+ reviews AND
- 2-3 of your primary keywords are stable on page 1 for at least 2 weeks

Raise in increments of $1-2 every 5-7 days. Watch conversion rate. If CVR drops more than 3 percentage points, hold the price.

**Floor rule:** never price below your break-even point. If 15% below page 1 average puts you in the red, rethink the product — not the math.

## Phase 3 — PPC Campaign Structure

Set up all four campaigns on day 1. Total daily budget: $80-105/day across all campaigns.

### Campaign 1 — Auto (Discovery)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Automatic |
| Daily budget | $25/day |
| Default bid | $0.85 |
| Targeting | Close match ON, Loose match ON, Substitutes OFF, Complements OFF |
| Run duration | Weeks 1-2 minimum, then harvest and review |

Purpose: let Amazon's algorithm find converting search terms you haven't thought of. After 2 weeks, pull the search term report and mine it for winners. Move converting terms to Campaign 2.

### Campaign 2 — Manual Exact (Core Keywords)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Manual — Exact match |
| Daily budget | $35/day |
| Starting bid | $1.25 (adjust based on suggested bids) |
| Keyword count | 5-10 keywords maximum |
| Keywords to target | Your 5-10 highest-volume, most relevant money keywords |

Purpose: rank for your primary keywords fast. These keywords drive the majority of organic rank movement. Monitor ACOS daily in weeks 1-2. This is your most important campaign.

Add all keywords as negatives in Campaign 1 once they're in this campaign.

### Campaign 3 — Manual Broad (Long-tail Discovery)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Manual — Broad match |
| Daily budget | $20/day |
| Starting bid | $0.75 |
| Keyword count | 10-15 phrases |
| Keywords to target | Broader variations and long-tail versions of your core terms |

Purpose: find converting long-tail terms at lower cost per click. Add negative keywords weekly from search term reports to stop wasted spend.

### Campaign 4 — ASIN Targeting (Product Targeting)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Manual — Product targeting |
| Daily budget | $15/day |
| Targets | Competitor ASINs with 3.8-4.2 stars AND under 100 reviews |
| Secondary targets | Complementary products (things bought alongside yours) |

Purpose: appear on weaker competitors' product pages. Shoppers browsing a 3.9-star product with 80 reviews are already in buy mode — steal that traffic.

Find weak competitors: search your main keyword, filter by review count and rating, copy ASINs.

## Phase 4 — ACOS & Budget Management

**Break-even ACOS formula:**

  Break-even ACOS = (Selling price - COGS - FBA fees) / Selling price x 100

Example: sell at $35, COGS $8, FBA fees $7 → break-even ACOS = ($35 - $8 - $7) / $35 x 100 = 57%

**ACOS targets by phase:**

| Phase | Weeks | Acceptable ACOS | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | 1-4 | Up to 80% | Buy rank, not profit |
| Growth | 5-8 | At break-even | Break even on PPC, profit on organic |
| Mature | 9+ | 20-35% | PPC profitability |

**Kill rules — pause a keyword immediately if:**
- 20+ clicks, 0 sales (at any phase)
- ACOS >120% after 6 weeks with no downward trend
- Impressions consistently 0 (bid too low or not relevant)

**Weekly spend cap recommendation:** $500-700/week total in weeks 1-4. Do not increase until you understand what's converting.

## Phase 5 — Keyword Ranking Strategy

Focus beats breadth. Most beginners try to rank for 20 keywords at once and rank for none of them.

**The approach:**
1. Pick 3-5 primary keywords — the ones with the highest search volume that your listing can realistically rank for
2. Direct all Campaign 2 (Exact) budget toward these terms
3. Track positions daily in Helium 10 Keyword Tracker
4. Once 2-3 of those keywords are stable on page 1, expand to the next tier

**The keyword honeymoon:** Amazon gives new ASINs a temporary organic ranking boost in the first 2-3 weeks. Use this window by running aggressive PPC immediately at launch — not after you've "set things up". The honeymoon ends whether you use it or not.

**Organic rank mechanics:** Amazon sees consistent sales on a search term → assumes the listing is relevant → pushes it higher organically. PPC drives the initial sales that signal relevance. More PPC sales on a keyword = faster organic movement on that keyword.

## Phase 6 — Review Velocity Strategy

Without reviews, nothing else matters. Conversion rate collapses under 10 reviews. Target: 15+ reviews by day 60.

**Lever 1 — Amazon Vine (highest priority)**
- Cost: ~$200 enrolment fee
- Result: up to 30 reviews from Amazon's trusted reviewer pool
- Requirement: Brand Registry must be active
- Enrol immediately once Brand Registry clears — do not wait
- These are your most credible early reviews

**Lever 2 — Request a Review Button**
- Location: Seller Central → Orders → click each order → "Request a Review"
- Or automate via a tool like Jungle Scout or Helium 10's Follow-Up
- Timing: set to trigger 7-15 days after delivery
- Sends Amazon's standardised review + seller feedback request
- This is TOS-compliant — the "Request a Review" flow is explicitly allowed

**Lever 3 — Insert Cards**
- Physical card in packaging
- QR code links to your product listing page (NOT directly to the review page — that is a TOS violation)
- Message: "Scan to register your product / share your feedback"
- Keep it clean and simple — aggressive review begging reads as desperate

**What not to do:**
- Fake reviews: account suspension, permanent ban risk
- Facebook review groups: against TOS
- Review swaps with other sellers: against TOS
- Offering incentives for reviews: against TOS

## Phase 7 — 30 / 60 / 90 Day Benchmarks

Use this table to assess health at each milestone. If you're below benchmark, identify which lever to pull — it's almost always listing, price, or keyword targeting.

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviews | 5-15 | 15-30 | 30-50 |
| Page 1 keywords | 1-2 | 3-5 | 5-8 |
| Daily organic sales | 1-3 | 3-8 | 8-15 |
| ACOS | 60-80% | 40-60% | 25-40% |
| Listing CVR | 8-12% | 10-15% | 12-18% |
| Total units sold | 30-60 | 100-180 | 250-400 |

CVR (conversion rate) = orders / sessions. Find this in Seller Central → Reports → Business Reports → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by ASIN.

## Phase 8 — Red Flags & When to Stop

Do not let optimism override the data. These are hard stops.

| Signal | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days live, <10 total sales with PPC running | Listing or product problem | Fix listing first. If no change after 2 weeks, question the product. |
| ACOS >120% at week 6 with no improvement | Keyword or bid strategy broken | Audit search term reports, pause non-converters, restructure campaigns |
| CVR <5% consistently | Traffic is fine — listing is the problem | Main image is almost always the culprit. Split test a new hero image. |
| Review score drops below 4.0 | Product quality issue | Do not scale. Pause ads. Fix the product. |
| Stock running out before 90 days | Inventory planning failure | Rush a reorder. Running out of stock destroys ranking — it can take weeks to recover. |

## Phase 9 — Weekly Search Term Report Routine

20 minutes every Monday. Non-negotiable. This is what separates profitable FBA sellers from ones who burn their budget.

**Step-by-step:**
1. Seller Central → Reports → Advertising Reports → Search Term Report
2. Download the report for the past 7 days
3. Sort by sales (descending)
4. Any search term with 2+ sales → add to Campaign 2 as exact match keyword
5. Any search term with 10+ clicks and 0 sales → add as negative exact in that campaign
6. Any search term with 1 sale and ACOS <80% → watch for another week before adding
7. Review bids on Campaign 2 keywords: if ACOS trending down, hold bids. If >100% after 3 weeks, cut bid by 20%.

**Tools that help:** Helium 10's Cerebro and Magnet for keyword research, Keyword Tracker for daily rank monitoring. These are worth the subscription cost in the launch phase.
