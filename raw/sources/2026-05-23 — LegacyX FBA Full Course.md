What Type Of Business Is Right For You
This lesson explains how to choose the right business structure for your goals.
Sole proprietorship: simple, full control, personal liability

### ✅ Barcodes and GTIN Exemptions Guide
> URL: https://legacyxfba.thinkific.com/courses/take/new-course/lessons/66485396-barcodes-and-gtin-exemptions-guide

Barcodes and GTIN Exemptions Guide
Barcodes confuse a lot of people. Here’s what you need to know:
UPC / GTIN codes: Normally, Amazon requires these to create a listing. We teach you to get a GTIN exemption so you don’t need UPCs. This avoids ownership issues (no one can claim your listing by proving they own the UPC).
FNSKU barcodes: After creating your dummy listing, Amazon generates an FN SKU barcode. This is what goes on each product unit so Amazon can track it. You get this from your Manage Inventory page under “Print Item Labels.” The text doesn’t matter – only the barcode and number do.
Shipping labels: When creating a shipping plan, Amazon gives you shipping labels. These go on the boxes (cartons) containing your units for delivery to Amazon.
Pallet labels: Rarely needed. Most products ship in cartons without pallets because pallets add cost and weight.
Refer to the barcode guide PDF in the course assets for examples. If confused, check the FAQ or ask on Zoom calls.
