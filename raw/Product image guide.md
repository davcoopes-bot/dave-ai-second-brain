This comprehensive guide to uploading and managing product images on Amazon explains the requirements for adding compliant images that help customers evaluate your products.

For detailed steps on how to upload images, go to [Upload images](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/help/hub/reference/external/GUGPCYWV2P8KVFUT?locale=en-AU).

Product images are essential for selling on our site. Every product requires at least one compliant main image. We recommend that you upload at least six additional images and one video to help customers evaluate products effectively.

High-quality images provide the following benefits:

- Help customers assess products accurately
- Improve product discoverability in search
- Increase customer confidence in purchasing decisions
- Reduces product returns due to mismatched expectations

## Product image requirements and selection

Every product on Amazon must have a main image. We recommend that you have at least six images and one video of the product. To learn more about image types, go to [Image variants](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/help/hub/reference/external/G8HQAHPDXA9ANPH4?locale=en-AU).

To create the best shopping experience, we select and arrange product images from multiple selling partners. This ensures that each product has sufficient high-quality images to support informed purchasing decisions. We may also modify images that you submit to meet the product image requirements.

You can upload additional images to your product listings at any time. To maximise the chance of your images being displayed, ensure that they comply with our product image requirements.

**Important notes**

Uploading an image doesn’t guarantee that it will be displayed. To ensure that our customers have the best experience, we may select images different from those provided by brand owners.

If your image is selected for display, it can take **up to 24 hours** after you’ve uploaded it to appear on the product detail page.

The following restrictions apply to images that do not meet our technical and visual standards:

- Images that fail to meet technical requirements cannot be uploaded to our system.
- Images that fail to meet product image requirements or violate our [Selling policies and seller code of conduct](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/help/hub/reference/external/G1801?locale=en-AU) may be removed.
- If there is no main image that meets the requirements, we may suppress the product listing from search until a compliant main image has been provided.

## Main product images

The first image on the detail page is called the “main image”. This image is also shown to customers in search results.

Your main product image must meet the following requirements:

- Accurately represents the product as a realistic, professional-quality image. The image should accurately represent the real scale, quantity and colour of the product.
- No placeholders: This includes a temporary placeholder as the first image uploaded defines the product identity. Using a temporary placeholder can cause your intended image to be blocked from upload.
- Have a pure white background (RGB colour values: 255, 255, 255) to create a consistent shopping experience for customers across search and product detail pages.
- Only a limited number of product types are allowed to have a lifestyle image as a main image (with no text or extra logos). For more information, go to the [Product page style guide](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/gp/help/external/G200270100?locale=en-AU).
- Show the product as 85% of the image.
- No text, logos, borders, colour blocks, watermarks or other graphics covering the product or in the background. Products with multiple pieces must be relative in size, with no one piece enlarged.
- Show the entire product within the frame of the image, not cutting off any part.
- Don’t show accessories or any props that aren’t included with the product and could confuse the customer.
- Only show the product once in the image, for example, front only, and not both the front and the back in the same image.
- Only show one unit of the product and any accessories that are included with the product.
- Don’t include packaging, unless it’s an important product feature (for example, a carrying case or gift basket may be included).
- Don’t show any part of a mannequin, regardless of the mannequin’s appearance. This includes being clear, solid-colour, flesh-toned, framework or on a hanger.
- Show a single shoe, facing left, at a 45-degree angle (applies to all footwear).
- Show clothing accessories or multi-packs flat, without a model.
- Show on a model if the product is clothing in adult sizes. The model must be standing, not sitting, kneeling, leaning or lying down. However, we encourage representing various physical mobilities and models who use assistive technology, such as wheelchairs and prosthetics.
- For multi-packs, assortments and clothing, read further guidance in the help pages below:
	- [Image guidelines for clothing](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/gp/help/external/G200498950?locale=en-AU)
		- [Multipack imaging standards](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/gp/help/external/G94GSFMC79RSLDBY?locale=en-AU) (not clothing)

## Requirements for all product images

All product images, main or otherwise, must meet the following requirements:

- Accurately represent the product that you’re selling.
- Match the product’s title.
- No nudity or sexually suggestive images. This includes both models and drawings.
- Children’s and baby undergarments, leotards, swimwear and similar form-fitting items must be photographed lying flat and without models.
- No images of customer reviews, five-star imagery, claims (for example, free shipping) or selling partner-specific information
- No text or pricing details (swatch image only)
- No Amazon logos or trademarks, variations, modifications or anything confusingly similar to our logos and trademarks. This includes, but is not limited to, any words or logos with the terms “Amazon”, “Prime”, “Alexa” or the Amazon Smile design.
- No badges used on Amazon, or variations, modifications or anything confusingly similar to such badges. This includes, but is not limited to, “Amazon’s Choice”, “Premium Choice”, “Amazon Alexa”, “Works with Amazon Alexa”, “Best seller” or “Top seller”.

## Troubleshooting

Images that fail to meet technical and product image requirements listed above cannot be uploaded to our system.

For troubleshooting help, consult the following sources:

- To learn more about the type of image variants allow, go to [Image variants](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/help/hub/reference/external/G8HQAHPDXA9ANPH4?locale=en-AU).
- If you’re creating your own images, you may find our [imaging products](https://www.amazon.com.au/pstudioipv) and [photography videos](https://www.youtube.com/c/AmazonImaging) useful.
- To view your images that failed to meet technical or our compliance requirements, go to **Catalogue**, select **Upload images** and then click [Submission status](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/imaging/upload/status). Click **Issue reason** to review the report.
	- Note that you must fix the Issues highlighted on the report before you re-upload. To learn more about issue reasons that are non-compliant with our policies, go to [Image issues](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/gp/help/external/GT8RSE9S9NK4LP6Z?locale=en-AU).
- To learn why images might not appear on the product detail page, go to [Images not appearing on the detail page](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/gp/help/external/G17811?locale=en-AU).
