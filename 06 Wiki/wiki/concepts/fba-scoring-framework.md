## Summary

The framework used by Dave and Claude to evaluate FBA niche opportunities. A 5-category scoring system (100 points total) with a pre-filter gate that must be passed before scoring begins. Developed and iterated through the FBA research sessions.

---

## Pre-Filter Gate (Pass All 5 or Stop)

Before any research, a product must pass all five criteria:

| Criterion | Why It Matters |
|-----------|---------------|
| Price can credibly land $20–70 | Below $20: margins destroyed by FBA fees. Above $70: slower purchase velocity, higher return risk |
| Not electronics, fragile, or liquid | Complexity in returns, damage, compliance. Soft goods and simple hard goods only |
| No dominant brand ownership | If Nike, Apple, or a strong category brand owns the keyword, don't compete |
| Under ~2 lbs | FBA fee tiers increase sharply above 1–2 lbs. Keep fees manageable |
| No legal/safety minefield | Avoid supplements, children's products, electrical, medical — compliance cost is high |

**All 5 must pass.** One fail = move on. No partial credit.

## Scoring Rubric (100 Points)

### Revenue Potential (25 pts)

**Score 25:** 3+ sellers on page 1 doing $10k+/month each
**Score 15–20:** 1–2 sellers at $10k+, rest between $5–10k
**Score 5–10:** Most sellers under $5k/month — market too small
**Score 0–5:** Thin revenue, dominated by one player

*Why it matters:* You don't need to beat the leader — you need the category to have enough total revenue that your slice is meaningful.

---

### Competition Gap (25 pts)

This is the most important category. Review count is the primary kill criterion.

**Score 20–25:** Average top-10 reviews < 200
**Score 15–20:** Average top-10 reviews 200–500
**Score 10–15:** Average top-10 reviews 500–800
**Score 5–10:** Average top-10 reviews 800–1,500
**Score 0–5:** Average top-10 reviews > 1,500 (effectively closed)

**Review ceiling benchmarks:**
- < 200 avg: Easy entry. Competitive in 3–4 months.
- 200–500 avg: Manageable. 6–9 months review-building. Enter on sub-keywords first.
- 500–1,000 avg: Hard. Requires long-tail strategy and real PPC budget.
- 1,000+ avg: Closed unless existing brand or war chest.

---

### Search Demand (20 pts)

**Score 20:** Main keyword 10,000–60,000 monthly searches
**Score 15:** 5,000–10,000
**Score 10:** 60,000+ (too competitive) OR 3,000–5,000 (too small)
**Score 5:** < 3,000

*The sweet spot is 10k–60k: enough buyers to build real revenue, not so many that the giants have taken over.*

---

### Differentiation Opportunity (15 pts)

**Score 15:** Obvious product improvements visible in reviews + weak branding across competitors + weak listings
**Score 10:** Fixable product problems but mid-tier branding/listings are decent
**Score 5:** Some room but top sellers are well-rated and hard to out-product
**Score 0–3:** Buyers satisfied (4.5+ star avg), no structural complaint to solve

*The best signal: a top seller's reviews contain a repeating complaint that their listing doesn't address.*

---

### Business Viability (15 pts)

Net margin is the metric. Calculated as:
```
Selling Price
- COGS (landed, including freight to FBA)
- FBA fees
- PPC spend (% of revenue — use 17–20% for manageable markets, 25–30% for saturated ones)
= Net margin
```

**Score 15:** Net margin > 40%
**Score 10:** Net margin 30–40%
**Score 5:** Net margin 20–30%
**Score 0:** Net margin < 20%

## Verdict Thresholds

| Score | Verdict | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 90–100 | ✅ Green Light | Move to sampling immediately |
| 75–89 | ✅ Green Light (with conditions) | Validate key assumptions, then sample |
| 60–74 | 🟡 Investigate More | Specific data gaps need filling before deciding |
| 45–59 | 🔶 Caution | Only pursue if specific conditions are met |
| < 45 | ❌ Pass | Move on — don't revisit without major market change |

## The Helium 10 Research Sequence

For each product that passes pre-filter:

**Step 1 — Black Box (product database)**
Find products in the category meeting minimum criteria (revenue, review count, price, star rating). Identify candidates.

**Step 2 — Magnet (keyword research)**
Map the keyword landscape. Find the head term, the sub-terms that are growing, and the long-tail entry points with lower competition counts.

**Step 3 — Xray (competitor deep dive)**
Pull page 1 data for the target keyword. Calculate avg revenue, avg reviews, avg price. Identify the review moat. Look at revenue distribution (top-heavy vs. spread).

**Step 4 — Market analysis**
- Revenue distribution (is it winnable outside the top 2?)
- Review ceiling (how bad is the moat?)
- Price consistency (is there a premium tier open?)
- Review quality gap (what are buyers complaining about?)
- Seasonal flag

**Step 5 — Alibaba cost estimate**
- Landed COGS including freight
- FBA fees
- PPC assumption
- Net margin calculation (headline and stress-tested)

**Step 6 — Competitor listing audit**
Top 3 competitors: title quality, main image, bullet points, brand presence. Rate overall listing quality (Weak / Average / Strong).

---

## Critical Insight: Data Is Simulated Until Validated

All research runs on simulated H10 data as a first pass. This shows whether the opportunity shape is right. **But no capital should be committed until real Helium 10 Xray data confirms the numbers.**

The simulation is for direction. Real data is for decisions.
