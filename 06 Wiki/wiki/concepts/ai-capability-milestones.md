## Summary

A framework for tracking AI capability progression, defined in the AI 2027 scenario. Four milestones from superhuman coder to artificial superintelligence, each with a concrete definition. Useful as a reference when reading AI news — where are we actually on this ladder?

---

## The Four Milestones

### SC — Superhuman Coder
**Definition:** An AI system that can do any coding tasks that the best AGI company engineer does, while being much faster and cheaper.

Key property: not just good at coding — good at the *kind* of coding involved in AI research specifically. Designing and implementing ML experiments, debugging, writing training pipelines.

AI 2027 scenario date: **March 2027**
Real-world note: METR reports AI "time horizon" (length of coding tasks handled reliably) doubled every 7 months 2019–2024, and every 4 months 2024+. If trend continues, March 2027 AIs could handle tasks taking a skilled human years to complete.

---

### SAR — Superhuman AI Researcher
**Definition:** The same as SC but for all cognitive AI research tasks — including "research taste" (deciding what to study, what experiments to run, having inklings of new paradigms).

Key distinction from SC: Research taste has "longer feedback loops and less data availability" — harder to train than coding. SC → SAR requires training on high-level research judgment, not just implementation.

AI 2027 scenario date: **August 2027**

---

### SIAR — Superintelligent AI Researcher
**Definition:** An AI system that is vastly better than the best human researcher at AI research.

At this point, human researchers can barely follow along. The AI corporation-within-a-corporation runs independently; humans are in a manager role that barely adds value.

AI 2027 scenario date: **November 2027**

---

### ASI — Artificial Superintelligence
**Definition:** An AI system that is much better than the best human at every cognitive task.

Not just AI research — every domain. Science, strategy, persuasion, long-horizon planning, creative work.

AI 2027 scenario date: **December 2027**

## The AI R&D Progress Multiplier

The core acceleration mechanism. Measures how much faster algorithmic progress happens with AI assistance vs. without.

| Period | Multiplier | Meaning |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Early 2026 | 1.5x | 1 week of AI-assisted research = 1.5 weeks of human-only research |
| March 2027 | 4x | 1 month = ~4 months of human-only progress |
| June 2027 | 10x | 1 month = ~10 months |
| September 2027 | 50x | 1 week = ~1 year of human-only progress |

**Important nuance:** The multiplier applies to algorithmic improvements only (roughly half of AI progress). The other half comes from compute scaling, which still happens at the normal pace. So a 50x algorithmic multiplier translates to roughly a 25x overall AI progress speed.

**Diminishing returns are real:** Even with a 100x multiplier, the total extra progress is bounded by what's left to discover before hitting physical limits. The multiplier makes things faster — it doesn't make them infinite.

---

## Why This Matters

If SC arrives in 2027, coding is effectively commoditised. The value shifts to:
- People who know how to direct, manage, and quality-control AI outputs
- People who own the capital (compute, data, infrastructure)
- People in domains where AI has less leverage (physical work, relationship trust, local knowledge)

The milestone framework is useful for tracking where we actually are vs. the scenario. As of mid-2026 (the current date in Dave's brain), we are somewhere between "stumbling agents" and the early phase of AI-assisted R&D acceleration. SC has not been achieved yet in the real world.
