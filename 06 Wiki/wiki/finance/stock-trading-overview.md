## Individual Stock Rule (Formalised — 2026-05-06)

> The catalyst is the exit trigger, not the price.

Before buying any individual stock:
1. **Define the thesis** — why are you buying this specifically?
2. **Define the exit condition** — what would invalidate the thesis?
3. **Set the catalyst** — if there's an upcoming event (announcement, results, flow test), that event is your decision point

If the thesis is invalidated → sell immediately regardless of the loss. Do not hold hoping for recovery.
If a catalyst is pending → hold until the result, then decide within 24 hours. No dragging it out.

ERA and EXR (Mongolian thesis) are the case studies. Costly lessons. Don't repeat.
