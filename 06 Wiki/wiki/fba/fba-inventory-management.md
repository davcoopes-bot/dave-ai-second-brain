## Summary

Practical inventory management for an Amazon FBA private label seller: order quantities, removal notices, return rate benchmarks, and how to track inventory status. Most of this becomes second nature after the first order cycle — but getting the first order sizing right is critical for cash flow.

---

## Order Quantities (First Order)

| Price Point | Recommended First Order |
|-------------|------------------------|
| $25–$50 | 250–300 units |
| $75+ | ~150 units |

Logic: Lower-priced items sell faster — you need more buffer. Higher-priced items have slower velocity, so you don't need as many to generate early data and stay liquid.

---

## Removal Notices

Amazon sends removal notices when inventory is scheduled for **automated disposal** (charged if not actioned). This happens when stock sits unsold past certain thresholds.

**How to disable auto-disposal:**
Settings → Fulfillment by Amazon → Automated Removals settings (accessible via top right Account Settings button)

Turn this off so you're in control of removals rather than Amazon auto-disposing stock you paid for.

## Understanding Inventory Status

Amazon inventory status explained: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/201723860#wjz_cjc_jcb

Your listing may show **out-of-stock** even when inventory is on its way — this is a fulfillment centre distribution delay affecting Prime availability visibility by region. Normal. It resolves as stock moves through the network.

## Return Rates

| Category | Normal Return Rate |
|----------|-------------------|
| Most products | 1–5% |
| Clothing / textiles | Up to ~15% |

**What to do if return rate is high:**
- Check return reasons (available under FBA Orders menu — but note: reasons are often unreliable, buyers pick anything)
- If there's a consistent issue, address it with your supplier on the next order
- High return rates from a product defect should be escalated to the supplier immediately — don't let it compound into negative reviews

**What happens to returned products:**
Amazon may: restock as new, mark as refurbished, liquidate, or dispose — depends on product condition and your account settings.

## Storage Capacity

Amazon limits how much inventory you can send in at once based on your IPI score and account history. Storage capacity bidding explanation: https://youtu.be/2G4gG-KBN_0?si=WC851cF8BURGaha_

Early on this is unlikely to be a constraint — it only bites once you have significant inventory volume.

---

## Inventory Notification Channels

When inventory arrives at Amazon:
- Email notification from Amazon
- Shipping plan updates in Seller Central
- Manage Inventory screen shows availability

Note: data on same-day inventory and sales is unreliable. Amazon's systems take up to a week to fully close and reflect accurate data. For anything time-sensitive, wait 48+ hours before acting on numbers.
