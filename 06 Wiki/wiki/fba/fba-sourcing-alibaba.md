## Summary

The standard sourcing path for Amazon private label: source from Chinese manufacturers on Alibaba, ship direct to Amazon FBA warehouses. Alibaba is legitimate but supplier due diligence is non-negotiable — most sourcing failures come from skipping samples or QC inspection. The 8-step process below is the full workflow from research to scale.

## 8-Step Sourcing Process

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Research product — demand, competition, and margin confirmed first |
| 2 | Search Alibaba — filter by Trade Assurance + Verified Supplier |
| 3 | Contact 3–5 suppliers — compare price, MOQ, shipping terms |
| 4 | Order and test samples from top 2–3 — non-negotiable |
| 5 | Calculate full landed cost (COGS + shipping + customs + FBA fees) |
| 6 | Place trial order — 100–300 units to validate quality at production scale |
| 7 | Ship to Amazon FBA — FNSKU labeling + packaging per Amazon specs |
| 8 | Monitor + scale — reviews, ads, restock, expand |

## Supplier Vetting Checklist

- [ ] Trade Assurance enabled (**required** — do not proceed without it)
- [ ] Verified Supplier badge (**required**)
- [ ] High rating + solid review history
- [ ] Responsive communication (test response time before committing)
- [ ] Willing to send samples before bulk order
- [ ] Clear MOQ and payment terms
- [ ] Provides pre-shipment video or accepts third-party QC inspection

## The Five Critical Sourcing Mistakes

| Mistake | What It Costs | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Skipping samples | Thousands in unsellable inventory | Order from 2–3 suppliers; test for a week. $50–150 saves $5k. |
| No QC inspection | Defective batch hits FBA before you know | QIMA or V-Trust (~$200–400). Never release final payment until inspection passes. |
| Single supplier | Zero leverage; one failure = stockout | Line up a backup supplier before you need one |
| Ignoring certifications | Listing suspension, inventory seized | Research cert requirements before sourcing, not after |
| No product modification | Generic = copyable by competitors tomorrow | Make at least one material, feature, or design improvement |

## LegacyX Supplier Outreach Protocol (Thinkific Course)

**First contact — go direct, not RFQ:**
LegacyX explicitly recommends against using RFQ for your first contacts. Go direct to individual verified suppliers instead. Contact 15–20 suppliers with 4.8+ rating and a strong US market focus.

**Message template (first contact):**
- State your quantity (e.g. 200 units)
- State your target price (e.g. $1.50/unit)
- Note that freight is paid by you
- Request a sample order

**Payment progression:**
1. First orders: credit card via Alibaba Trade Assurance (protected)
2. Once trust is established: switch to wire transfers

## LegacyX Negotiating Masterclass

- Alibaba prices to foreign buyers are often inflated 200–400% (confirmed in multiple lessons)
- Contact 20–30 suppliers individually — not just RFQs
- **State how many units you want at your target price** — don't ask for quotes. Asking for quotes puts you in a weak position. Stating your number puts you in control.
- Get **separate line-item prices** for: product, packaging, and accessories
- Mention that you sell on Amazon — this gives suppliers context for why you need competitive pricing
- Students regularly achieve **45–60% net margins** by negotiating confidently
- Mindset: don't limit yourself by assuming you can't get big discounts. The spread between Alibaba list price and achievable price is real.

## Key Sourcing Tools & Tactics

**RFQ (Request for Quotation):** Alibaba's tool to broadcast your requirement to multiple suppliers at once. Faster than cold-messaging one by one. Note: LegacyX recommends going direct first — use RFQ only after direct outreach.

**Trade Assurance / Escrow:** Always pay through Alibaba's platform. Never wire money outside the platform to a new supplier.

**3PL Staging Strategy:** Consider routing first shipment through a US 3PL warehouse before sending to FBA:
- Inspect inventory on arrival
- Repack/relabel if needed
- Send to FBA in batches (avoids storage overload)
- Cost: ~$30–50/month for US staging/return address

## Landed Cost Calculation

Your COGS is just the factory price. The full landed cost includes:

| Component | Typical Cost |
|-----------|-------------|
| Factory/manufacturing price | Baseline |
| Packaging and labeling | + varies |
| International freight (sea) | + varies (cheaper, slower) |
| International freight (air) | + 4–5x sea cost (faster, expensive) |
| Customs brokerage fees | + $50–150/shipment |
| Import duties/tariffs | + 5–25% of declared value (look up HTS code) |
| FBA inbound shipping | + varies |
| **Total landed cost** | **Factory price + 20–40%** |

**Rule:** LegacyX target is 35–45% margins. 30% is minimum acceptable — anything below that risks ending up in the red after final costs settle. Don't plan to hit 30%; plan to hit 35–45% and let slippage land you at 30%.

## Trial Order Strategy

Start with 100–300 units on first order:
- Validates product quality at production scale (not just sample quality)
- Tests market response (listing + early sales data)
- Lets you learn the full logistics workflow before committing large capital
- Reveals any labeling or FBA prep issues early

## Payment Timeline

1. Pay deposit (~30%) to confirm order placement
2. Supplier runs production
3. Third-party QC inspection (before final payment)
4. Pay balance (~70%) only after inspection passes
5. Freight forwarder arranges shipment to FBA

## LegacyX Profitability Notes (Thinkific Course)

- **Target 35–45% net margins** — not just 30%. Plan for 35–45% so after final costs you land at 30%+ at minimum.
- **FBA fees are based on volumetric (package) weight**, not just product weight. The shipping company charges whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight.
- **Sizing thresholds matter enormously**: Crossing from small standard to large standard (18" threshold) or into oversize dramatically increases FBA fees. Use Helium 10 calculators with **exact supplier package dimensions** — not the product dimensions alone.
- Higher ticket items can stay profitable below 30% margin if the net dollar return per unit is strong. A $100 item at 25% margin = $25 net. A $15 item at 35% = $5.25 net. Margin % alone doesn't tell the full story.

## LegacyX Opening Offer Tactic (1688.com)

This tactic from the LegacyX FAQ gets you a real Chinese wholesale price as your negotiating anchor:

1. Find the product on Alibaba (via RFQ or image search)
2. Search the **same product images** on 1688.com (China's domestic wholesale market)
3. Note the average price listed in Yuan → convert to USD
4. Use ~**80% of that converted price as your opening offer** on Alibaba

**Why it works:** 1688.com shows the actual domestic factory price that Chinese buyers pay — not the inflated export price Alibaba shows foreign buyers. Alibaba suppliers expect negotiation; this gives you a data-backed anchor.

**Note:** Only mainland China buyers can purchase directly from 1688.com. Use it as a **price reference only**, not a sourcing channel.

**Inflation confirmation from LegacyX Thinkific course:** Alibaba prices shown to foreign buyers are often **2–4x inflated** vs the real factory price. This is expected — always negotiate hard from the 1688.com anchor price.

## Supplier Volume Rule

LegacyX course recommendation: **contact at least 30 suppliers** before selecting one. This is more than the 3–5 commonly cited in generic guides. More outreach = better negotiating leverage, more price data, and faster filtering of unreliable suppliers.

The 30-supplier rule isn't about having 30 ongoing relationships — it's about casting wide enough to find the best 2–3 to get samples from.

Also: **Alibaba holds supplier payments for 30 days** (Trade Assurance). This protects you — always pay through Alibaba, never direct bank transfer. If something goes wrong, Trade Assurance gives you recourse.

## Done For You Sourcing Alternative (bosssourcing.com)

LegacyX in-house sourcing partner: **bosssourcing.com** — agents find products, negotiate pricing, and perform quality checks. Cost: 5–7.5% markup on product cost.

**How it works:**
- Prepare full product specs, target price, MOQ, and any required certifications before contacting
- Agents source and negotiate on your behalf — supplier names not shared (they are the middleman)
- No Alibaba Trade Assurance on these orders — use **qcadvisor.com** for independent QC inspections
- Zero tolerance for disrespect from buyers — unprofessional behaviour gets you banned

**Alternate sourcing service (less tested):** gescmanagement.com (Golden Eagle Supply Chain). **Not approved for their shipping service** — use for sourcing only if at all. LegacyX coaches have not tested their shipping.

For shipping, use **Freight Shark** (LegacyX vetted DDP freight forwarder) — see [[wiki/fba/fba-shipping.md]].
