## ⚠️ CRITERIA REALITY CHECK

**12 batches. 90+ keywords. Zero passes under LegacyX criteria ($200k + ≤500 reviews).**

This is not a keyword strategy problem — it's a structural market reality. The data shows a near-perfect inverse correlation: categories with $200k+/mo revenue have 5,000–30,000+ avg reviews. Categories with ≤500 avg reviews generate $15k–$80k/mo at most.

**What this means:**
- The LegacyX $200k + ≤500 criteria describes an emerging market — one where demand is large but competition hasn't yet built review walls
- Established physical goods categories will never satisfy both criteria simultaneously
- The only way to find compliant products is to search for trends/formats that are genuinely new

**What did pass the OLD criteria ($50k + ≤1,000 reviews):**
- GLP-1 cooling cases — emerging product type (accessory to brand new drug class)
- Creatine gummies — new delivery format for established supplement

**Path forward options:**
1. Revisit GLP-1 cooling case — passes old criteria, legitimate emerging market, simple product
2. Search 2025/2026 trending product categories specifically (new gadgets, new accessories, new formats)
3. Accept that LegacyX criteria is aspirational/conservative and use $50k + ≤1,000 as the working threshold

## The Review Ceiling Rule

The single most important criterion. All 80+ niches tested confirm the same pattern:

| Review Range (top 10 avg) | Entry Difficulty | Notes |
|--------------------------|-----------------|-------|
| **< 300** | Open — move fast | Window closing as you read this |
| 300–700 | Manageable | 6–9 months to compete |
| 700–1,000 | Hard — needs strong brand | Still viable with proper execution |
| 1,000–2,000 | Near-miss territory | Sub-niche research needed |
| 2,000+ | Closed | Don't enter |
