## Key Metric: The Review Ceiling Rule

The single most important criterion in FBA research. Everything else can be worked around — thin margin can be fixed by pricing up, weak demand can be targeted via sub-keywords. But a deep review moat on all keywords is effectively a closed door.

**Useful benchmarks:**
- < 200 avg (top 10): Easy entry. Competitive inside 3–4 months.
- 200–500 avg: Manageable. 6–9 months of review building. Enter on sub-keywords first.
- 500–1,000 avg: Hard. Requires long-tail strategy and real PPC budget.
- 1,000+ avg: Closed unless you have an existing brand or war chest.
