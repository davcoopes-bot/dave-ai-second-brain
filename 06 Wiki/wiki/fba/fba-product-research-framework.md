## Summary

The data-driven methodology for finding a viable private label product. Built around a winning product formula with specific numerical targets, a 7-step research workflow, and a pre-validation micro-test before committing to large MOQ. This complements the existing FBA scoring framework — use the scoring framework to score candidates; use this page for the research methodology that gets you to candidates in the first place.

## The Winning Product Formula

| Factor | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Monthly keyword searches | 3,000+ (seasonal stability, not spikes) | Check Keepa for price/trend history |
| Competitor reviews | **<300** (conservative) / <500 (moderate) | Use <300 — the tighter filter |
| Competitor star rating | Below 4.6 | Room to compete on quality |
| Price range | $20–$60 | $20–$50 sweet spot |
| Gross margin | 30%+ after FBA fees + PPC | Model with sensitivity checks |
| ROI | 100%+ | After all landed costs |
| Size/weight | Small and light | Lower FBA fees and shipping |
| Brand dominance | Avoid if 2–3 sellers own 70%+ of page 1 with 10k+ reviews | No way in |

**On the review threshold:** The Helium 10 source uses <500 reviews. The Savvy FBA source uses <300. Use <300 as the conservative and defensible filter — entering at higher competition levels increases launch risk.

## 7-Step Research Workflow

**Step 1 — Map Your Niche**
Pick 3–5 audience segments you understand. Find the problems they pay to solve. Turn those into seed keywords.

**Step 2 — Quantify Demand**
Use Amazon search suggestions, Product Opportunity Explorer, and search frequency rankings. Check pricing history with Keepa — not a single snapshot.

**Step 3 — Audit Competitors**
Review top 10 results for your main keyword. Record: price, review count, rating, listing quality, brand dominance. Flag gaps — weak images, no A+ Content, thin bullets, poor packaging.

**Step 4 — Model Unit Economics**
Run the FBA Revenue Calculator with real factory quotes (packaging + inspection + freight + duty included). Target 30% gross margin and 100% ROI. Then run sensitivity checks:
- What if price drops 10%?
- What if PPC costs rise 20%?
Both scenarios must still work.

**Step 5 — Pre-Validate With Micro Tests**
Before committing to large MOQ, validate keyword fit and conversion signal:
- Run a small Sponsored Products campaign on a similar ASIN or FBM test batch
- ✅ CTR >0.6%
- ✅ Conversion rate >12%
These are early green lights before you've spent serious money.

**Step 6 — Design Your Differentiation**
Read 200–300 competitor reviews and Q&A. Find recurring complaints and unanswered feature requests. Turn the top 3 insights into concrete improvements. **If the improvement isn't visible in your main image and first 2 bullet points, it's not strong enough.**

**Step 7 — Plan Supply & Cash Flow**
Confirm lead times, MOQ flexibility, inspection process. Forecast inventory weeks of cover. Set reorder points = lead time + safety stock.

## Product Attribute Checklist

Good private label candidates share these characteristics:

- [ ] Small and lightweight (lower shipping + FBA storage costs)
- [ ] Simple to manufacture (fewer QC issues at scale)
- [ ] Durable (fewer returns, lower return rate)
- [ ] Easy to differentiate (materials, features, bundling, size variants)
- [ ] Steady demand — not purely seasonal
- [ ] Avoid: fragile, highly regulated, requires complex assembly

## Differentiation Approach

1. Read 200–300 competitor 1–3 star reviews — find the top 3 recurring complaints
2. Read Q&A section — find unanswered feature requests
3. Identify concrete improvements (material upgrade, added feature, better bundling, different size or colour)
4. Validate: Is the improvement visible in your main image and first 2 bullet points? If not, it's not differentiated enough.

## LegacyX Research Tips (Operational Add-ons)

Additions from the LegacyX FAQ that sharpen the research process:

- **Price gaps** indicate different consumer markets — a product selling at both $15 and $45 serves different buyers. Know which segment you're entering.
- **Chinese seller dominance is irrelevant** — they sell under brand names same as anyone else. Don't filter out a niche just because Chinese sellers are present.
- **Consistent return reasons** that can't be fixed by suppliers = risk. Check 1-star reviews for recurring unfixable complaints before committing.
- **Return rates >3–5%** may indicate a product quality issue. Factor this into your margin model — returns eat margin.
- **Value-add features must be visible in your main image and first 2 bullet points** — if you can't show the differentiation front-and-centre, it's not strong enough to justify the complexity.
- **Don't overcomplicate bundles/features** — adding unnecessary accessories signals you couldn't find a clear differentiator on the core product.
- **Launch max ~3 variations** on a first product — more than that fragments your review count and complicates inventory management.

## LegacyX Criteria (H10 Research — Official Thresholds)

From the LegacyX Thinkific course (Product Research module), the actual H10 research criteria used:

| Criterion | LegacyX threshold | Our H10 scraper | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|-------|
| Niche revenue | **$200K+** | $50K | ⚠️ Likely refers to total niche across all keywords, not single keyword search |
| Avg price | **$25+** | $15–80 sweet spot | Similar |
| Avg reviews | **≤500** | ≤1,000 | LegacyX is stricter |
| Revenue per seller (avg) | **$5K+** | Not tracked | New metric |
| Brand domination | Top 2–3 sellers <50% of revenue | Not tracked | New metric |
| Pre-ad margin | **30%+** | 30%+ | Same |

**Why the $200k vs $50k discrepancy matters:**
The $200k threshold likely refers to the *total niche* revenue across all related keywords — not one specific long-tail keyword search. Our H10 scraper queries specific keywords and finds $10k–50k because we're too narrow. The fix: either (a) search broader category keywords, or (b) validate by checking multiple related keywords and summing total niche revenue. The scoring framework pre-filter of $50k per keyword is not wrong — it's just a different measure.

**What to add to H10 validation going forward:**
- Revenue per seller (avg) — can calculate from H10 Xray output
- Brand domination check — top 2–3 sellers % of total page 1 revenue

## Seller Sprite — Niche-Down + Rabbit Hole Strategy

From LegacyX Thinkific course, the Seller Sprite research methodology:

- **Filter for:** FBA, US/China/HK locations
- **Niche-down strategy:** Tighten price or review ranges progressively to load new product sets. Combine category filters to maximise unique results.
- **Rabbit hole strategy:** Find one product → research related products and variations within that niche → generates unlimited concepts to validate
