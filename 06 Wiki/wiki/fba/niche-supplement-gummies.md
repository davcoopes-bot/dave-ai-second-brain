## Why This Works — The Format Thesis

Most supplement categories are brutally competitive. But supplements in **new delivery formats** create temporary windows:
- Creatine powder: 40,000+ reviews on top sellers — closed
- Creatine gummies: 253 avg reviews — open
- Magnesium capsules: tens of thousands of reviews — closed
- Magnesium gummies: 994 avg reviews — barely open
- NMN capsules: competitive — closed
- NMN gummies: 776 avg reviews — open

The same customer who buys creatine gummies buys magnesium gummies. Stack all three under one brand → repeat purchase and cross-sell from day one.

## Supplement-Specific Requirements

This is NOT standard FBA sourcing. Additional requirements:

- **GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) certified manufacturer** — non-negotiable baseline
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** for every production run — lab-tested proof of ingredient purity
- **Supplement Facts panel** compliant labelling — required on all US dietary supplements
- **Structure/function claims only** — "supports muscle recovery" ✅, "treats muscle damage" ❌
- **Amazon Dietary Supplements category approval** — must be ungated before listing goes live
  - Requires: GMP certification docs, invoices from manufacturer, possibly COA
- **FDA prior notice** if importing from outside the US (avoided by using US manufacturer)

## Manufacturer Strategy

**Use a US GMP manufacturer for launch.** Higher COGS but eliminates FDA import risk, quality risk, and lead time blowouts. Move to Chinese manufacturer only after brand is validated.

**Target US manufacturers (contact all for quotes):**
- **Makers Nutrition** — makersnutrition.com — popular Amazon PL choice, gummy specialist
- **NutraCap Labs** — nutracaplabs.com — low MOQ, works with small brands
- **Gummy Industries** — gummyindustries.com — gummy specialist

## Brand Requirements

Supplement gummies require a **premium, clean brand identity** — not a generic Amazon PL look.
- Brand name: needs to feel clean and health-adjacent, works across all 3 SKUs and future supplements
- File USPTO trademark (Class 5 — dietary supplements) — $250–350
- Consider Amazon IP Accelerator for faster Brand Registry access (~$500–2,000 legal fees)
- Logo and visual identity: budget $200–500
- Brand Registry unlocks: A+ Content, Brand Analytics, Amazon Vine, Sponsored Brands

> **Note:** MsWLL is the current dummy brand name used for GTIN exemption only. The actual supplement brand name needs to be decided before launch.

## Launch Sequence

1. Creatine gummy listing live → enrol Amazon Vine ($200 — up to 30 reviews)
2. Auto PPC campaign → run 2 weeks, harvest keyword data
3. Broad match campaign on winners from auto
4. Exact match campaign on proven converters
5. Launch ACOS target: 50–70% (buying rank, not profit — first 4–6 weeks)
6. Steady-state ACOS target: <30%
7. At ~30 reviews: raise price from launch price ($22–23) to full price ($26)
8. Once ranked + 4.2+ stars: add magnesium glycinate SKU → same sequence
