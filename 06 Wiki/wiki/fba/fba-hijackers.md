# FBA — Hijacker Defence

> **Category:** fba
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-23
> **Sources:** [[wiki/sources/src — LegacyX FBA FAQ.md]]

## Summary

A hijacker is a third-party seller who jumps onto your listing and takes the Buy Box — meaning they get the sale even though it's your product and listing. Hijacking is common for any listing gaining traction. This page covers how to detect them, respond, and prevent them long-term.

---

## How to Tell If You Have a Hijacker

Check your listing. If the **Buy Box (featured offer)** shows a brand name or store name that isn't yours → hijacker.

**Exceptions that aren't hijackers:**
- "Amazon Warehouse" or "Amazon" selling your product = Amazon reselling a returned/refurbished unit eligible for sale. Not a hijacker — they're moving inventory Amazon acquired via returns.
- If you've lost the Buy Box with **no other sellers present** → this is a pricing issue, not a hijacker. Check: pricing health menu, ad campaigns, account health, and account info screen.

---

## Response Protocol

Act in this order when you spot a hijacker:

1. **Pause your ads immediately** — no point paying for clicks when a hijacker is winning the sale
2. **Send cease and desist letters** — as per LegacyX course material
3. **Use Nawprotect** — hijacker removal service available on the LegacyX services page (https://inhouse.legacyxfba.com/)
4. **Use Brand Registry infringement tools** — only if you have a fully registered trademark; report hijacker as selling a fraudulent product
5. **Apply for Amazon Brand Transparency Program** — if fully trademarked; gets specialised QR code barcodes applied by Amazon that make hijacking almost impossible

---

## Prevention

**Best protection: Brand Registry + fully registered trademark.** Without this you're fighting with limited tools.

With trademark + Brand Registry you get:
- Amazon's infringement reporting tools
- Brand Transparency Program eligibility (QR code barcodes)
- Brand Gating (some categories)

**Without trademark (interim measures):**
- Custom packaging visible in your main image — if your packaging is unique and clearly branded, a hijacker can't easily list the same product with matching images
- Unique product inserts or branding elements that make a generic knock-off visually identifiable as fake

## The Buy Box — Related Dynamics

**Losing the Buy Box when raising prices** (common problem):
This usually happens because you've:
- Increased price too far above market average
- Increased price too far in the last 30 days
- Set "your price" above "list price" in the listing editor
- Increased price in too large an increment

Fix: Revert to the last price at which you had the Buy Box → wait a few days → make smaller incremental increases.

**Slashed sale price (crossed-out original price):**
Amazon only shows this if your listing has had a higher price for a set period of time. Simply having a "list price" vs "your price" difference doesn't automatically trigger the sale display.

**Organic vs. sponsored sales — how to separate them:**
Amazon doesn't have a native tool for this. Method: look at the same time frame in your FBA dashboard (total sales) and your Campaign Manager (ad sales) → subtract ad sales from total. Reliable for data older than 48 hours; same-day data is unreliable (Amazon systems lag 6–24 hours).
