> **Category:** fba
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-23

## Summary

The launch sequence and review strategy that bridges the gap between "listing is live" and "listing has momentum." Getting the order of operations wrong here wastes PPC budget and risks review removal. The fundamentals: get social proof before spending on ads, maintain a safe review rate ratio, and understand the specific timing quirks of Amazon's review system.

---

## Launch Sequence (Order Matters)

1. **Get your first friends & family review** — before any ads run
2. **Turn on automatic PPC campaign** — generates early sales velocity
3. **Wait until first review appears on the listing** — this can take 48 hours to 2 weeks depending on account age
4. **Then turn on manual campaigns** — per the PPC ad strategy

> Why this order? You're offsetting early ad-driven sales with social proof before scaling spend. Running manual campaigns before any reviews = paying for clicks that won't convert.

## How Many Units to Launch

| Price Point | Launch Quantity |
|-------------|----------------|
| $25–$45 | 200–300 units |
| $75–$100+ | 100–150 units |

These quantities give enough inventory to build velocity and gather data without over-committing capital before you know the sell-through rate.

## Review Strategy

### The Safe Stacking Ratio
- **Target**: 1 review per ~5 sales
- **Stay below**: ~20% overall review rate to avoid Amazon flags and removals
- **First milestone**: 10–25 reviews — this is the threshold that establishes enough trust for conversion

### Friends & Family Reviews
- Use the **Super URL method** (PDF in LegacyX Assets Drive)
- Reviewers must not have been on the same WiFi as you in the last 6+ months
- Reviewers should post a maximum of 2 products (avoid patterns)
- Spread timing — don't have multiple friends review on the same day
- **Unverified reviews** are not recommended — low weight, eventually removed by Amazon

### Review Timing
- Standard review posting time: within 48 hours
- New accounts: can take up to 12–14 days for reviews to appear
- Plan your launch timeline around this — don't start ad campaigns expecting reviews to appear the same day

### Helium 10 Follow-Up Email System
- Better than insert cards for review generation (insert cards walk a TOS tightrope)
- Can't explicitly solicit reviews per Amazon TOS — use the automated email sequence to keep buyers engaged and let them choose to review organically

## Custom Packaging — Dos & Don'ts

### DO:
- Get the **source AI file** from your designer (so you can make future edits)
- Get the **box die/cut** from your supplier (flat box template with measurements)
- Make it **clean and minimal** — simple designs convert better
- Use **colours that complement or contrast** the product (not match)
- Include **FNSKU barcode** on the packaging
- Use **matte finish**
- Include your **brand name**

### DON'T:
- Use **gloss finish**
- Put a **product image on the packaging**
- Use **full-colour packaging** on large/bulky items (cost not justified)
- **Spend more than $0.30–$1 per unit** (for most products)
- Use **white packaging** — blends into the main image white background

### When Custom Packaging Is Worth It
Best ROI for products where aesthetics matter: home decor, toys, gift items. Custom packaging can also enhance your main image if it's visible in the shot — this makes it harder for hijackers to copy your listing.

---

## Insert Cards

Generally not worth the cost for most products. Acceptable uses:
- Assembly instruction QR codes
- How-to video links
- Product manuals

**Not acceptable per Amazon TOS:**
- Explicit review solicitation ("Please leave us a 5-star review")
- Anything that skews buyer perspective or offers incentives for reviews
