## Key Terminology

| Term | What It Means |
|------|---------------|
| **EXW (Ex Works)** | Supplier's price to their factory door only. You pay freight from that point. Use this to see the true product cost with no shipping bundled in. |
| **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** | Freight forwarder handles everything from pickup — international freight, import/export documentation, customs duties, and final delivery to your Amazon FBA warehouse. This is what you want. |
| **Volumetric weight** | Box dimensions converted to a weight equivalent. Shipping companies charge whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight. Important: negotiate EXW and know your box dimensions before you can calculate true landed cost. |
| **FBA fee tiers** | Small standard, large standard, small oversize — defined by package dimensions. Crossing the 18" threshold into large standard or oversize dramatically increases FBA fees. |
| **Freight forwarder** | The third party who manages your international shipment. They handle logistics, customs, duties, and delivery to Amazon. You don't manage this yourself. |

## Shipping Strategy — The LegacyX Method

### Step 1: Negotiate EXW with your supplier
- Ask for EXW pricing when getting quotes — this is the cost to the supplier's factory door only
- EXW lets you see the **true product cost** separated from shipping
- You can't accurately calculate your margin until you know where EXW ends and freight begins

### Step 2: Ship DDP with a vetted freight forwarder
- DDP means the freight forwarder takes your product from the supplier's door all the way to Amazon's warehouse
- They handle: pickup, export paperwork, international freight, customs clearance, import duties, and final delivery
- You avoid liability and complexity — the forwarder owns the logistics problem
- LegacyX vetted DDP service: **Freight Shark** (see below)

### Step 3: Use correct dimensions for fees and cost planning
- **Unit dimensions** affect FBA fees — know your product's packaged size before choosing a product
- **Carton dimensions** affect shipping cost — get these from your supplier early
- Use Helium 10's FBA fee calculator with **exact supplier package dimensions** to plan margins accurately
- Amazon auto-moves inventory between warehouses after check-in — you have no control over speed or destination

## FBA Fee Tiers — Size Thresholds

Amazon charges FBA fees based on the size tier of your packaged product. Crossing thresholds increases fees significantly.

| Tier | Size Limit | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Small standard | ≤15" × 12" × 0.75" | Lowest fees |
| Large standard | ≤18" on longest side | Significant fee jump vs small standard |
| Small oversize | >18" on any side | Dramatically higher fees |
| Medium/large oversize | Much larger | Very high — avoid for first product |

**Key rule:** Know your product's packaged dimensions before committing. If you're close to the 18" threshold, verify with your supplier — a few centimetres can add meaningful cost per unit.

## Landed Cost — Shipping Components

Shipping cost depends on whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight of the carton.

| Component | Pricing Basis |
|-----------|--------------|
| International sea freight | Volumetric or actual weight per carton — sea is slower, cheaper |
| International air freight | Volumetric or actual weight — 4–5x sea cost, faster |
| Customs/import duties | % of declared product value (look up HTS code for your product) |
| Customs brokerage | Usually included in DDP; confirm with forwarder |
| FBA inbound shipping | Amazon receives from forwarder — included in DDP delivery |

Amazon auto-distributes inventory across warehouses after check-in. This is not controllable.

## Shipping Plan Process (Overview)

**Note:** The detailed step-by-step shipping plan lessons (Creating A Shipping Plan x2, Booking An Inspection) had network errors during scraping. Watch these directly in Thinkific before creating your first shipment.

General flow based on available content:
1. Confirm final unit count with supplier
2. Get exact carton dimensions and weights from supplier
3. Create shipping plan in Seller Central (point to your dummy listing ASIN)
4. Book freight with Freight Shark (DDP to Amazon warehouse)
5. Book QC inspection — either through LegacyX partner or qcadvisor.com — before final payment release
6. Release final payment to supplier after inspection passes
7. Freight departs; Amazon receives and checks in inventory
