## Summary

Everything you need in place before you sell: separate business accounts, LLC formation, NAICS code, licenses, liability insurance, and tax basics. Most of this is one-time setup — get it right at the start and you never have to revisit it. Australian sellers have additional steps (Wise account, W-8BEN — see [[wiki/fba/fba-aus-seller-setup.md]]).

## Business Name

**This is a zero-impact decision — treat it that way.** Customers never see your LLC name. It has no effect on your brand, your Amazon listings, or anything that matters commercially.

Use a simple formula: **initials + "Distribution"** or **initials + "Wholesale"** (e.g. "DC Distribution LLC"). Make this a 5-minute call and move on. Do not spend days deliberating — it's the wrong place to spend energy.

## Brand Name (Amazon Brand — Separate From LLC Name)

Your Amazon brand name is what appears on your listings — customers DO see this. It affects conversion rates: listings marked "Generic" convert badly because buyers perceive them as cheap, even if the product is identical.

**Naming rules — avoid all of these:**
- Common words or dictionary words (e.g. "Ultra Marine" — untrademarkable)
- Simple misspellings of real words
- Category-specific terms (e.g. a brand called "Coffee Pro" for coffee gear)
- Anything already trademarked — check USPTO before submitting
- Anything that exists on Amazon, Google, or GS1

**What works best:**
- Random nonsense names (e.g. "Schmerley") — unique, approvable, no legal issues
- Pet name with vowels removed — quick to generate, feels personal
- Invented words that sound brandable — short, distinctive, easy to say

**Check your brand name before submitting to FBA Pro (in this order):**
1. **Google** — search the name; look for existing brands or businesses
2. **Amazon** — search the name; make sure it's not already a registered brand
3. **USPTO** https://www.uspto.gov/ — check for existing trademarks
4. **GS1** — confirm no one already owns that brand on the product identifier system

**Getting approved:** FBA Pro (now at www.agency-studios.com) uses laser-etched product images to unlock brand approval on Amazon. This is a GTIN exemption + brand approval service. Choosing a non-compliant name and having to redo it costs extra — get this right the first time.

**Trademark filing (when ready):** https://www.primetmservices.com/ — needed for Brand Registry (A+ Content + hijacker protection), not required for initial brand approval.

> **MsWLL status:** Current placeholder brand submitted for GTIN exemption. MsWLL is deliberately nonsensical — passes all naming criteria. ✅

## Business Structure

**Should you form an LLC?**
Yes — protects personal assets, makes accounting cleaner, and Amazon treats business accounts more favourably for approvals. LegacyX recommended partner: Northwest LLC (affiliate: shrsl.com/2qhem-1hzb-kp67 — 1 year free registered agent service)

**⚠️ Set up your LLC *before* your Amazon Seller Central account.** If you form the LLC after creating your Seller Central account, you'll need to update your legal entity — Amazon may trigger extra re-verification steps (identity docs, bank statement, etc.) that can delay your account. Do the LLC first, even if it adds a few days..

**NAICS code when asked for licenses:**
Use **455219** — "All other general merchandise retailers"

**What licenses do you need?**
Depends on your state. The LegacyX course has links to state-specific tools. If in doubt, contact the state body that issues sales licenses directly. For Australian sellers this is less relevant — focus on the federal/Amazon side first.

## Separate Accounts — Non-Negotiable

Set up dedicated accounts for your Amazon business:

| Account | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **Email address** | Keeps Amazon notifications, supplier emails, and customer messages out of your personal inbox. Domain doesn't matter (Gmail is fine) — just needs to be separate. |
| **Bank account** | Keeps business money separate from personal money. Doesn't need to be a formal business account — a separate personal account in business-only use is acceptable to start. |
| **Credit card** | Separate card for all business expenses. Makes accounting and tax prep dramatically easier. |

## Liability Insurance

- Amazon requires liability insurance **once you hit $10k/month revenue** — but not all products/categories require it
- If you're outside the US, you do NOT need US-based insurance — local provider is fine; check Amazon's policy for minimum coverage requirements
- LegacyX has a liability insurance partner on their services page: https://inhouse.legacyxfba.com/
- Amazon policy reference: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G200386300

## Amazon Account Setup

**Account type:** Always use a **Pro Seller account ($39.99/month)**. Individual accounts limit you to 40 units/month and no access to PPC or Brand Registry. Pro is the minimum for real FBA.

**Seller account setup specifics:**
- Set up your LLC before registering — changing structure later triggers extra Amazon re-verification steps
- Match your business name exactly — case-sensitive, no special characters
- **For brand status question:** Select **"yes" for private label**
- **For UPC/barcode questions:** Answer **"no"** — you'll be getting a GTIN exemption
- Storefront name doesn't matter — customers see your brand name, not your storefront name
- Complete ID verification steps carefully — delays here slow everything down
- Keep business bank, credit card, and email separate from personal

**Inactive account fix:**
If Amazon disables your account for inactivity before you've made any sale, it's standard — they only count selling as activity. Go to Account Health → submit the requested verification document (or update a credit card with the same card) → reactivates in 3–7 days.

**Using an existing account:**
If you have an old account (arbitrage, wholesale) with good health, use it — established accounts get approvals faster than new accounts. Opening a second account requires Amazon approval and a "legitimate business reason" plus unique EIN/bank info.

## Taxes

- Get a CPA who specialises in business taxes and understands ecommerce — this is non-negotiable. Don't try to wing it.
- Amazon 1099 policy: see https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/T86AHE3YMNCB4P4
- For Australian sellers: W-8BEN form is mandatory to claim the AU-US tax treaty rate (0% withholding vs 30% default). See [[wiki/fba/fba-aus-seller-setup.md]].
