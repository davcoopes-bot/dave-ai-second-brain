## Summary

Everything an Australian seller needs to set up on Amazon US. The setup is entirely doable remotely — no US residency, no US address required. Three things are non-negotiable before the first sale: a US bank account (Revolut (or Wise)), the W-8BEN tax form, and a Professional Seller account. Everything else can be sorted progressively as revenue scales.

## Non-Negotiables (Do Before Launch)

| Item | What | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Revolut account (or Wise)** | Free US bank details (routing + account number) | Amazon US won't pay to Australian accounts. Need US-routed account. Dave uses Revolut — confirmed compatible. |
| **W-8BEN form** | Certify foreign seller status in Amazon Seller Central | No W-8BEN = Amazon withholds **30%** of all earnings |
| **Professional Seller Account** | $39.99/month | Required for private label — worth it from day one |
| **Seller Identity Verification (SIV)** | Passport + proof of address | Amazon requirement at account creation |

## Tax — The Full Picture

### US Federal Income Tax

The AU-US tax treaty protects you. FBA income is generally **not subject to US federal income tax** — you pay in Australia only. This is because FBA warehousing doesn't create a "permanent establishment" for foreign sellers under the treaty.

### US Sales Tax

- **Amazon collects and remits sales tax** as marketplace facilitator in all states — you don't handle the collection
- **BUT**: Having FBA inventory in US warehouses creates physical "nexus." Some states (CA, TX, NY) require registration and filing even when $0 is due
- **At launch: don't stress about this.** Sort compliance once generating real revenue. Use TaxJar or Avalara (~$20–50/month) when ready.

### Australian Tax

- Amazon US income is **assessable income in Australia** — declare on your Aus tax return
- **GST**: Not applicable on US marketplace sales (they're exports). Register for GST once total AUD turnover hits $75k

## Operations (Fully Remote)

- Ship direct from Chinese supplier → Amazon US FBA warehouses — standard practice, no need to be in the US
- **US return address**: Required for customer returns. US freight forwarder or 3PL handles this for ~$30–50/month

## Summary: What's True vs. What Needs Nuance

| Assumption | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| "We can sell from Australia" | ✅ Correct |
| "Only pay tax in Australia" | ✅ Mostly true — AU-US treaty covers income tax |
| "Sales tax is Amazon's problem" | ⚠️ Amazon collects it, but state registration may be required at scale |
| "We can use our Aussie bank account" | ❌ No — need US routing/account number (Revolut, Wise, or Payoneer) |
| "We need to be in the US" | ✅ No — everything managed remotely |

## Cost of Getting This Wrong

- No W-8BEN → 30% withholding on all earnings
- Wrong bank setup → can't get paid at all
- Ignoring sales tax registration at scale → penalties and audit risk in US states
