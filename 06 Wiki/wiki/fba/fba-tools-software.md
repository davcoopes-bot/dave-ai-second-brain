## Summary

The three dominant Amazon research tools for private label sellers, plus supporting software used through the FBA lifecycle. We're already using Helium 10 — this page documents the landscape for context and covers the supporting tools LegacyX uses across launch, ads, and operations.

---

## Core Research Tools

### Helium 10 ⭐ (Our Tool)
**Link:** https://i.helium10.com/Vx17Mj (LegacyX affiliate)

**Strengths:**
- Largest user base — community, tutorials, and integrations everywhere
- Best for listing building and keyword optimisation
- Has automated PPC tool (Adtomic — additional cost)
- Mid-level account covers most FBA needs

**Weaknesses:**
- Steep learning curve — many features, easy to get lost
- Automated PPC is an extra cost beyond the base plan
- Lower plans restrict tool usage (Xray limits, etc.)

**Verdict:** Industry standard. Mid-level plan is the minimum for serious research. We use this — it's the right choice.

---

### Seller Sprite
**Link:** https://www.sellersprite.com/affiliate/BIC30 (LegacyX affiliate)

**Strengths:**
- Has a **free tier** — useful for beginners before committing budget
- Uses Google Trends integration for market analysis
- Reverse ASIN and keyword research tools
- **Unique feature:** connects Amazon product images to Alibaba suppliers (useful for sourcing research)

**Weaknesses:**
- China-based — English translations can be awkward
- Paid plans get expensive quickly
- Limited customer support

**Verdict:** Interesting for the Alibaba image-matching feature. Free tier worth exploring for supplementary research.

---

### Jungle Scout
**Link:** https://get.junglescout.com/5e9cv72h1v52 (LegacyX affiliate)

**Strengths:**
- Easiest learning curve — best for beginners
- Opportunity Score gives a quick product viability rating
- Shows competitor inventory levels (useful for spotting stockouts)
- Keeps supplier info organised in one place

**Weaknesses:**
- No free plan or trial
- Limited advanced tools compared to H10
- Data updates slowly

**Verdict:** Good starting tool. We've already committed to Helium 10 and don't need to switch. Opportunity Score concept is useful — H10's equivalent is Cerebro/Black Box.

### Notes on Supporting Tools
- **LaunchFast** grades products from **A10 (best) to F1 (worst)**. B1 and above = worth pursuing. Built by LegacyX specifically for their students.
  - **Pricing:** $49/month for LegacyX students (vs $200/month general rate)
  - **Trial:** 3-day free trial with code **QUICKSTART3**
  - Use this to quickly grade product candidates before committing research time.
- **Seller-Shark** — all-in-one operations dashboard. Mentioned in the LegacyX "Other Recommended Software" module as a standalone tool (not just a PPC tool). Worth looking at as an alternative to having separate tools for tracking sales, inventory, and ads.
- **GETIDA** is worth setting up even early — Amazon regularly miscounts inventory, loses items, and misdirects shipments. GETIDA audits and files reimbursement claims. Works on a percentage of recovered funds (no upfront cost).
- **A2X + QuickBooks:** Use QuickBooks only if you're new (no prior sales). Use A2X + QuickBooks if you have existing Amazon sales — A2X pulls all historical sales data into QuickBooks.
- **Scale Insights** is a PPC automation alternative to H10's Adtomic — compare when you're actively running ads.
