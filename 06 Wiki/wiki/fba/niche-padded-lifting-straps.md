## The Differentiation Brief

Four specific complaints dominate mid-tier reviews (positions 4–8). These form the product brief:

1. **Straps too short for larger wrists / bigger hands** → spec longer strap length (24"+)
2. **Padding wears down after 3–4 months of heavy use** → spec denser, higher-durometer foam padding
3. **Stitching fails at the buckle under heavy load** → spec double-stitching at all stress points
4. **Wrist loop is stiff, uncomfortable for first 2–3 weeks** → spec softer break-in material or pre-conditioned loop

These are specific, sourceable fixes. Not "higher quality" vagueness — each one can be written into the Alibaba brief and verified on sample inspection.
