## Verdict

**❌ PASS.**

Real H10 data closes the case. "Silicone splatter screen" keyword generates ~$1,277/month total across 50 products. Rank #1 earns ~$110/month. Even taking 20% market share, that's ~$255/month — doesn't cover PPC, let alone justify launch capital.

The broader terms ("splatter guard", "splatter screen for frying pan") are real markets ($20–26k/month) but owned by stainless steel. OXO alone takes 36–47% share. Buyers on those terms aren't looking for silicone.

The differentiation brief was sound. The market just isn't there.

## The Two Specific Problems to Solve

**Problem 1 — Handle conducts heat.**
Appears in reviews of 7 out of 10 top products. Buyers burn themselves on metal handles during cooking. The fix is a full silicone wrap on the handle. This becomes the headline claim: "stay-cool handle, heat-resistant to 450°F."

**Problem 2 — Mesh clogs with grease.**
Traditional fine wire mesh traps cooking residue — multiple reviews mention throwing it out rather than washing it. Fix: open-weave or coarser silicone mesh, specifically designed to be dishwasher-safe. "Grease doesn't clog the mesh" becomes a bullet point.

Both problems are fixable at factory level. They're not exotic engineering — they're standard silicone fabrication. The issue is no mid-tier competitor has bothered to address them in their listing copy.

---

## Product Specification Brief

For Alibaba sourcing, the non-negotiables:
- **Full silicone wrap on handle** — must pass basic heat test (hold handle over gas flame for 30 seconds, confirm it stays cool to touch)
- **Coarser/open-weave mesh** — must clean under running water without scrubbing; dishwasher-safe is the claim
- **Accurate sizing** — label "fits 12-inch pans" based on actual pan measurement, not screen diameter
- **Solid one-piece construction** — no folding hinge (hinge failure is complaint #4)

Secondary: look for available size variants (10", 12", 13") — a 3-size set at a slightly higher price point is worth exploring.

## Listing Brief

**Title direction:** Lead with the product improvement, not the product type.
- Example: "Silicone Splatter Screen — Stay-Cool Handle, Dishwasher-Safe Mesh, Fits 10/12/13-inch Pans"

**Main image:** Pure white background hero shot — product at 85% of frame, full product visible, no text or graphics. Amazon requires this for most categories (G1881). ⚠️ Lifestyle images as main images are NOT allowed for standard kitchen products — this was incorrect in the original research.

**Secondary images (positions 2–6):** This is where you differentiate. Lead with a lifestyle shot — pan sizzling on a burner with the screen in use, steam/grease visually stopped. Every competitor uses white background only across ALL images. A lifestyle secondary image is still a strong differentiator.

**Bullet 1:** Stay-cool handle (solve complaint #1 directly)
**Bullet 2:** Dishwasher-safe mesh, grease doesn't clog (solve complaint #2 directly)
**Bullet 3:** Accurate sizing (solve complaint #3)
**Bullet 4:** One-piece construction, no hinge (solve complaint #4)

**Brand angle:** Even a basic brand (name, simple logo, A+ content with benefit graphics) puts you ahead of 8/10 competitors. No one has built a real brand in this space at mid-tier.
