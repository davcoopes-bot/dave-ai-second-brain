## Summary

Amazon PPC is non-negotiable during launch — new products don't rank organically. The strategy is a 3-tier campaign structure built around knowing your break-even ACOS before spending a dollar. During launch, you intentionally run at or above break-even ACOS to buy ranking, not profit. At scale, you bring TACOS down to 8–15% as organic kicks in.

## Step 1: Know Your Numbers Before Running Any Ad

**Break-even ACOS = your gross margin %**

> Example: Product sells for $20 with $13 in total costs → margin is $7 (35%). Break-even ACOS ceiling = 35%. Spend above it → every sale loses money.

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| ACOS | Ad spend ÷ ad revenue — advertising cost per sale as a % |
| TACOS | Total ad spend ÷ total revenue (organic + ads) — the real efficiency number |
| Break-even ACOS | Your gross margin % — max you can spend per sale |

**Target benchmarks:**
- **Launch phase**: Accept ACOS at or above break-even — you're buying ranking, not profit
- **Mature phase**: Target TACOS **8–15%** as organic ranking builds

## The 3-Tier Campaign Structure

| Campaign | Purpose | Bidding |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **Auto** | Let Amazon discover keywords — broadest discovery phase | Moderate bid |
| **Broad Match** | Target 2–3 word phrases around known keywords | Moderate bid |
| **Exact Match (Winners)** | Only proven, high-converting keywords migrated from above | 25–50% higher bid |

## The Workflow

1. **Launch**: Start Auto + Broad campaigns simultaneously
2. **Wait**: Run for 1–2 weeks minimum before touching anything
3. **Review**: Pull search term reports — find keywords converting at good ACOS (minimum 1,000 impressions before decisions)
4. **Graduate**: Move winners to Exact Match campaign with 25–50% higher bids
5. **Prune**: Add all non-converters as negative exact match keywords everywhere
6. **Repeat**: Weekly review initially → monthly at steady state

## Listing Quality Gate

**Check session conversion rate before scaling:**

| Conversion Rate | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| **>10%** | Strong listing — pour fuel on it |
| **5–10%** | Acceptable — room to improve |
| **<5%** | Fix the listing first. PPC on a bad listing burns money. |

If traffic is high and sales are low, the listing is broken. PPC can't fix it.

## Optimisation Checklist

- [ ] Know break-even ACOS before running first ad
- [ ] Separate campaigns by product and match type
- [ ] Minimum 1,000 impressions before making bid changes
- [ ] Use "Dynamic Bids — Down Only" (not Up/Down — too unpredictable)
- [ ] Add negative exact match keywords for anything wasting spend
- [ ] Check session conversion rate before scaling budget

## Common Mistakes

- Running ads without knowing break-even ACOS
- Expecting PPC to fix a bad listing (it won't — fix the listing first)
- Making bid changes on insufficient data (<1,000 impressions)
- Keywords too broad ("napkins") or too niche (zero search volume)
- Letting losing campaigns run because you forgot about them

## Budget Guidance (Launch Period)

- **Lean launch**: $300–$800 month one minimum
- **Full launch**: $500–$2,000 for aggressive launch
- Expect 4–8 weeks of advertising before organic rankings stabilise
- Start with Auto campaigns → optimise toward proven keywords once data exists
