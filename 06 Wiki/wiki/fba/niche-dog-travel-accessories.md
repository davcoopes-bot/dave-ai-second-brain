## Why It Fails

The top 3 sellers have 8,200–22,100 reviews. That is not a number you fight through in the normal review-building timeline. PPC spend to appear next to listings with 10k+ reviews would be astronomical. This is a mature category with entrenched winners.

Secondary: buyer satisfaction is high (4.4–4.6 stars). There's no obvious structural product problem to solve — the complaints are minor (lid leaks, size slightly off) and don't represent a fixable gap big enough to justify entry.
