## Summary

Full breakdown of what it costs to launch an Amazon private label product. Total budget range is **$3,000–$10,000** depending on niche, product price point, and launch aggression. The most common mistake: budgeting for inventory and forgetting everything else. Most new sellers underestimate total costs by 30–50%.

## Budget Scenarios

| Scenario | Budget | Profile |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Lean Start** | $3,000–$4,000 | Low-cost product ($3–6 COGS), 300 units, DIY photos, $300–400 PPC. Higher risk, less buffer. |
| **Healthy Start ⭐** | $5,000–$7,000 | Mid-range product ($6–10 COGS), 400–500 units, professional photography, $500–800 PPC, QC inspection. Recommended. |
| **Full Launch** | $8,000–$10,000 | Higher-priced product, 500+ units, full branding package, trademark filing, $800+ PPC. Best odds of success. |

**BigCommerce benchmarks (500-unit first order):**
- Conservative: ~$4,350 total
- Moderate: ~$8,140 total

## Hidden / Ongoing Fees (Silent Margin Killers)

These accumulate and destroy margins for unprepared sellers:

| Fee | Rate |
|-----|------|
| Referral fee | ~15% of every sale |
| FBA fulfillment fee | Varies by size/weight (~$3.00–$6.50/unit) |
| FBA storage fee | $0.75–$2.40/ft³/month |
| Aged inventory fee | $6.90/ft³ (kicks in at 181+ days — avoid at all costs) |
| Return processing fee | $2–$6/unit |
| Refund administration fee | Up to 20% of refunded amount |

## Landed Cost Breakdown

Your COGS is just the factory price. Full landed cost includes:
- Factory/manufacturing price
- Packaging and labeling
- International freight (sea = cheaper but slow; air = 4–5x cost)
- Customs brokerage fees ($50–$150/shipment)
- Import duties/tariffs (5–25% of declared value — varies by product category)
- FBA inbound shipping

**Rule of thumb:** Add 20–40% on top of factory price to get landed cost.

## Margin Target & Pricing Formula

**Target: 30–40% net margin** after Amazon fees, production, and advertising.

Work backwards:
- Manufacturing: $8/unit
- Shipping to Amazon: $2/unit
- Amazon fees (15% referral + FBA): ~$6/unit
- **Total cost: $16/unit**
- Target price at 25% margin: $21.33 → round to **$21.99**

If your unit economics don't model this before sourcing, don't source.

## Key Rules

1. **Run the FBA Revenue Calculator on every product before ordering** — no exceptions
2. **Budget PPC from day one** — visibility costs money at launch
3. **Don't skip QC inspection** — $150–300 before final payment prevents thousands in unsellable stock
4. **Factor landed cost, not just COGS** — the gap between factory price and shelf price is real
