## The Core Problem (Why to Avoid)

**Review moat is severe.** Top sellers have 2,800–3,100 reviews. Even position 9 has 185 reviews. In a niche where "resistance bands" as a head term has 142,000 monthly searches and 1,840 competing products, you're PPC-ing against entrenched sellers with massive review advantages. The PPC spend required to rank in months 1–6 would eat most of the margin.

**Peach Bands is a real brand.** The market leader has strong aesthetics, A+ content, a proper storefront, and 3,100 reviews. This is harder to compete against than generic Chinese private label.

---

## If Pursuing This (Conditions Required)

Only consider if:
1. Real H10 Xray on "non slip resistance bands" shows avg < 200 reviews on page 1
2. Silicone-grip inner lining sourced from Alibaba doesn't push COGS above $6 (keeping margin > 40% adjusted)
3. At least one other FBA product already running and cash-flow positive

Entry via non-slip variant: silicone-grip inner lining (Alibaba suppliers offer this), "non slip resistance bands" as primary keyword, skip the main "resistance bands" battlefield entirely.
