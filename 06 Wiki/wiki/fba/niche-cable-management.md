## The Fixable Problems

**Problem 1 — Lid doesn't stay closed.**
Most common complaint across top sellers. Lids either don't clip securely or the mechanism feels flimsy. Fix at factory level: proper clip mechanism, not a friction-fit lid.

**Problem 2 — Not enough cable exit holes / wrong size.**
Buyers run 4–6 cables through one box. Current designs often have 2–3 exit points and they're too narrow for multiple cables or thick surge protector cords. Fix: multiple exit holes, varying diameters.

**Problem 3 — Doesn't fit standard surge protector.**
Internal dimensions are wrong — buyers buy the box, find it doesn't accommodate a standard power strip. Fix: spec internal dimensions around common surge protector sizes before ordering.

**Problem 4 — Cheap plastic that cracks.**
Low-quality ABS on budget models cracks at corners after a few months. Fix: thicker ABS or add a matte finish (also signals premium).

---

## Product Specification Brief

For Alibaba sourcing:
- **Lid:** Clip or snap mechanism — must hold securely when opened from below (cable movement puts upward pressure on lid)
- **Cable exits:** Minimum 4 exit holes, at least 2 of them wide enough for a doubled cable bundle
- **Internal dimensions:** Must accommodate a standard 6-outlet surge protector (approx. 25cm × 6cm × 4cm) — confirm before ordering
- **Material:** Matte ABS or similar — signals premium vs glossy cheap plastic competitors
- **Size:** One size to start (large). Don't add a "small" variant until you have reviews.

## Listing Brief

**Title direction:** Clean and benefit-first, not a keyword dump.
- Example: "Cable Management Box — Secure Lid, Fits Standard Power Strips, Hides Up to 6 Cables"

**Main image:** Lifestyle shot — a clean, minimal desk setup with the box neatly concealing cables underneath. The before/after concept (tangled cables vs. hidden cables) is the visual hook. Every competitor uses white background only.

**Bullet 1:** Secure lid mechanism (solve complaint #1)
**Bullet 2:** Fits standard surge protectors + multiple cable exits (solve complaints #2 and #3)
**Bullet 3:** Premium matte finish, won't crack or yellow (solve complaint #4)

**Brand angle:** The "desk setup" community on TikTok and Reddit actively shares cable management content. A brand that leans into this aesthetic (minimal, clean, WFH-focused) has organic traffic potential beyond PPC. Even a simple brand with this direction beats every generic private label on page 1.
