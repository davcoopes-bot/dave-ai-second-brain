## Summary

Full FBA research session on the gym accessories niche. Three products passed the pre-filter (padded lifting straps, fabric resistance bands, wrist wraps). Two received deep research. Padded lifting straps scored 85/100 (Investigate More); fabric resistance bands scored 66/100 (Investigate More with caution). Wrist wraps summarised as a backup option.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Padded lifting straps** are the strongest gym accessories play — manageable review moat on sub-keywords ("padded lifting straps", "figure 8 lifting straps"), clear differentiation brief from 4 specific review complaints, 46% net margin, year-round demand.
- **Fabric resistance bands** have massive revenue ($153k/month page 1) but deep review moats (avg 950 reviews). Only viable via long-tail entry on "non slip resistance bands" with silicone-grip variant.
- **Wrist wraps** sit between the two on difficulty — worth standalone deep-dive if lifting straps sourcing proves tough.
- The gym accessories head terms are saturated; entry must be on specific long-tail sub-keywords.
- Differentiation brief for lifting straps: longer length, denser padding, double-stitched buckle, softer break-in feel.
