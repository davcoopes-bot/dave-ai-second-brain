## Key Takeaways

- **Launch sequence is specific**: Friends & family review first → auto campaign → wait for first review to appear → then manual campaigns. Getting this order wrong wastes ad spend before social proof exists.
- **Review stacking ratio**: 1 review per ~5 sales is the safe threshold. Maintain below ~20% overall review rate to avoid flags. Super URL method documented in assets drive.
- **FBA-Pro service replaces GTINs**: Avoid GS1/Barcodemania UPCs entirely — use FBA-Pro service for GTIN exemption + brand approval. Their UPCs are recycled and cause listing block errors.
- **1688.com pricing tactic**: Find product on Alibaba → search same images on 1688.com → convert Yuan to USD → offer ~80% of that price as opening offer on Alibaba. Non-mainland-China buyers can't purchase direct from 1688 but it's the reference price.
- **Custom packaging rule of thumb**: $0.30–$1 max per unit. Matte finish. Clean/minimal. No gloss, no product image on box, no white packaging (blends into main image background).
- **Hijacker response protocol**: Pause ads immediately (no point paying for clicks a hijacker wins) → cease and desist → Nawprotect service → Brand Registry infringement tools if trademarked.
- **Compliance documents**: Triggered by dummy listing creation for regulated products (baby, batteries). Get from supplier — child safety certs, SDS sheets. Reference: compliancegate.com.

## Contradictions with Existing Wiki

- **GTIN sourcing**: Existing compliance page recommends GS1-issued GTINs. LegacyX FAQ says avoid GS1/Barcodemania (recycled UPCs) and use FBA-Pro GTIN exemption service instead. These aren't fully contradictory — GS1 is the official Amazon-approved path, but LegacyX's FBA-Pro service routes around it via brand/GTIN exemption. Both valid, different approaches. ⚠️ Noted on compliance page.
- **Samples policy**: Existing sourcing page emphasises ordering custom samples. LegacyX says order stock samples from top 3 suppliers — custom samples cost more without added benefit at the selection stage. Minor difference in emphasis. LegacyX approach is more cost-efficient for early validation.
