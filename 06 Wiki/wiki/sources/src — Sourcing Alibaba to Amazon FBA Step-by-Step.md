## Summary

Forceget's 8-step process for sourcing products from Alibaba into Amazon FBA warehouses, summarised by Josh. Heavy emphasis on supplier vetting and protecting against fraud and quality risks. Practical and process-focused — less theory than other sources.

## Key Takeaways

- **8-step process**: Research → Find suppliers → Contact/compare (3–5) → Sample → Calculate full costs → Trial order (100–300 units) → Ship to FBA → Monitor/scale
- **Supplier vetting must-haves**: Trade Assurance enabled + Verified Supplier badge — both required, not optional
- **Use Alibaba's RFQ tool** — broadcasts requirement to multiple suppliers simultaneously; faster than cold-messaging one by one
- **3PL staging strategy** — consider routing first shipment through a US 3PL warehouse before FBA: inspect on arrival, repack if needed, send to FBA in batches to avoid storage overload
- **Never wire money outside the Alibaba platform** to a new supplier — always use Trade Assurance or Escrow
- **Starting budget: $2,200–$10,000** — consistent with other sources in this batch
- **Trial order first** — 100–300 units to validate quality at production scale before committing large capital
