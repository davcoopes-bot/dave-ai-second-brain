## Summary

Full cost breakdown for starting Amazon FBA private label, sourced from Be Bold Digital and summarised by Josh. Total budget range is $3,000–$10,000 depending on niche and launch strategy. The #1 reason new sellers fail is budgeting for inventory and forgetting everything else — especially ongoing fees that silently eat margins.

## Key Takeaways

- **Total budget: $3k–$10k** — Lean start ($3–4k), healthy start ($5–7k, recommended), full launch ($8–10k)
- **Mandatory: inventory ($1k–$3k) + shipping ($300–$1k)** — the two biggest line items
- **Professional Seller Account: $39.99/month** — required for private label from day one
- **PPC budget: $300–$800 in month one** — non-negotiable for launch visibility
- **30–40% net margin target** — if your model doesn't hit this, either price or sourcing cost is wrong
- **Hidden fees destroy unprepared margins**: referral fee (~15%), storage ($0.75–$2.40/ft³/month), aged inventory ($6.90/ft³ after 181 days), return processing, FBA labeling ($0.55/unit)
- **Quality control inspection: $150–$300** — QIMA or V-Trust before final payment; prevents inventory disasters
