## Key Takeaways

- **9-step process**: Brainstorm → Product attributes → Market research → Find suppliers → Logo/design/packaging → Fulfillment strategy → Choose manufacturer → Create listing → Optimise
- **64% of sellers started with under $5k; 25% with under $1k** — capital barrier is lower than perceived
- **58% of Amazon private label sellers profitable within first year**
- **One hero SKU can build a 6-figure business** — don't spread thin across catalogues early; master one product before expanding
- **Top sourcing mistakes**: skipping samples, no QC inspection (QIMA/V-Trust, ~$200–400), single supplier dependency, ignoring certifications, not modifying the product (generic = copyable)
- **Full launch budget scenarios**: Conservative (~$4,350) to Moderate (~$8,140) — most new sellers underestimate by 30–50%
- **Work backwards from 20–30% margin**: Example — $16 total cost → $21.99 selling price
- **Time commitment**: Months 1–3 near full-time (30–50 hrs/week); Year 2+ systematisable (5–15 hrs/week per product)
- **Amazon is the launchpad, not the destination** — most resilient brands use Amazon for reach while building direct customer relationships

## Concepts Introduced or Reinforced

- Landed cost (manufacturing + freight + customs brokerage + import duties = 20–40% above factory price)
- HTS code (look up before ordering to confirm duty rate)
- EORI number (required for UK/EU imports)
- CE/UKCA marking (EU/UK product compliance)
- Brand Registry (unlocks A+ Content, Vine, enhanced protection)
- Amazon Vine (free products to trusted reviewers — early review mechanism)
- 3PL vs FBA vs FBM (fulfillment options with trade-offs)
