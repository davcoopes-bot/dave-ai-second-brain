## Key Takeaways

- **Product research criteria**: Price $20–$70, 300+ monthly sales, <500 reviews (⚠️ more lenient than Savvy FBA's <300 — use <300 as the tighter filter), max 4 stars, Listing Health Score 5–7
- **Why private label over OA/Wholesale**: Own the Buy Box automatically, higher margins (direct from manufacturer), full listing control, brand equity building
- **Key cons of PL**: High upfront cost, 4–6 months minimum before live and selling, quality risk (you never touch the product)
- **7-step process**: Product research → Find supplier → Brand/logo → Create listing → Pricing strategy → FBA fulfillment → Launch
- **Pricing rule**: Stay within 20% of competitors' average. Sweet spot $20–$50.
- **Launch tactics**: PPC ads, external traffic (Instagram/Google), influencer outreach, lightning deals
