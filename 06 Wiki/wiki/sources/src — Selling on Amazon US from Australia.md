## Summary

Josh's research note on everything an Australian needs to set up on Amazon US. The core message: yes, Australians can sell on Amazon US without US residency, but getting the banking and tax paperwork right upfront avoids serious compliance problems later. The AU-US tax treaty provides strong protection on income tax, but the W-8BEN form and a US bank account are non-negotiable from day one.

## Key Takeaways

- **Need a US bank account** — Amazon US won't pay to an Australian account. Use Wise (recommended) or Payoneer for free US routing/account numbers.
- **File W-8BEN** — certifies foreign seller status; skipping it means Amazon withholds 30% of all earnings.
- **AU-US tax treaty covers income tax** — FBA income is generally not subject to US federal income tax; pay in Australia only.
- **Sales tax is mostly Amazon's problem** — Amazon collects and remits as marketplace facilitator in all states. But FBA inventory in US warehouses creates nexus; some states require registration/filing even when $0 is due. Don't stress at launch — sort compliance when revenue is real.
- **Ship direct China → Amazon US FBA** — never need to be physically in the US. A US 3PL (~$30–50/month) handles customer returns.
- **Australian tax return** — Amazon US income is assessable income in Australia. GST not applicable on US sales (export). Register for GST once total AUD turnover hits $75k.
