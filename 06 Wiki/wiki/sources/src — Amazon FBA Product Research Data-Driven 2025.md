## Summary

Savvy FBA's data-driven product research framework, noted by Josh as "the most practical product research framework I've found." Built around a 7-step workflow and a specific winning product formula with numerical targets. The pre-validation micro-test (step 5) is particularly valuable — running a small ad campaign before committing to large MOQ to confirm keyword fit and conversion signal.

## Key Takeaways

- **Winning product formula**: 3,000+ monthly searches, <300 competitor reviews, <4.6 competitor stars, $20–$60 price, 30%+ gross margin, 100%+ ROI, small/light, no 2–3 brands dominating with 10k+ reviews
- **7-step workflow**: Map niche → Quantify demand → Audit competitors → Model unit economics → Pre-validate → Design differentiation → Plan supply/cashflow
- **Pre-validation micro-test**: Run small Sponsored Products campaign before locking large MOQ. Green light signals: CTR >0.6%, CVR >12%
- **Differentiation standard**: Read 200–300 competitor reviews + Q&A. If the improvement isn't visible in your main image and first 2 bullets, it's not strong enough.
- **Sensitivity analysis**: Model what happens if price drops 10% or PPC costs rise 20% — margin must survive both stress tests
- **Reorder point formula**: lead time + safety stock

## Concepts Introduced or Reinforced

- TACOS (target 8–15% at scale)
- Pre-launch micro-testing (CTR >0.6% and CVR >12% as early green lights)
- Sensitivity analysis on unit economics
- Reorder point planning (lead time + safety stock)

## Contradictions with Existing Wiki Pages
