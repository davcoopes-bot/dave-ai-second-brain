## Summary

Comparative FBA research on three niches: dog travel accessories, bamboo kitchen utensils, and cable management (home office). Cable management scored 88/100 (Green Light). Dog travel accessories scored 51/100 (Caution — closed review wall). Bamboo kitchen utensils scored 37/100 (Pass — satisfied market, no differentiation angle).

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Cable management: 88/100 ✅ Green Light** — real revenue spread across 10 sellers, avg 808 reviews (achievable in 12 months), fixable product problems, weak listings, no dominant brand.
- **Dog travel accessories: 51/100 🔶 Caution** — review wall at avg 6,908 is effectively closed for a new entrant without a $30k+ launch budget. Revisit in 18 months.
- **Bamboo kitchen utensils: 37/100 ❌ Pass** — buyers are satisfied (4.4–4.7 stars), nothing meaningful to fix, search volume too small (6,800/month).
- The key lesson: **review moat is the primary kill criterion**. A niche with $50k/month revenue means nothing if you need 6,000 reviews to compete.
- Cable management opportunity: premium end ($32–38) is underdeveloped, competitor listings are all keyword dumps with product-only images. Even basic branding (name, logo, lifestyle shot) puts you ahead of 8/10 competitors.
- Cable management fix brief: secure lid mechanism, two cable exit hole sizes, enough internal space for a standard surge protector.
