## Summary

The LegacyX FBA Notion dashboard template for tracking product lifecycle from selection through to advertising and reviews. Includes a comparison of the three major Amazon research tools (Seller Sprite, Helium 10, Jungle Scout) with pros/cons for each. The product lifecycle board tracks: Product Development → PO Placed → Listing → Inventory → Advertising → Reviews → Canceled. Dave and Josh should duplicate this into their own Notion to use as a tracking layer.

## Key Takeaways

- **Product lifecycle tracking**: Full board with 9 stages and status columns for each. Template row includes: Product Name, Assigned, Due Date, Product Dev, Inventory Management, Listing, Advertising, Reviews, Lifecycle Stage, Calc, Notes.
- **Seller Sprite** has a free tier and connects Amazon images to Alibaba suppliers (useful for sourcing). Cons: China-based, awkward English translations, expensive paid plans.
- **Helium 10** is the industry standard with the largest user base. Better for listing building and optimisation. Has automated PPC tool (additional cost). Requires mid-level account for most features.
- **Jungle Scout** is easiest for beginners. Shows competitor inventory levels. Has Opportunity Score for quick rating. Cons: no free trial, data updates slow.
- **The "Adjustable Weighted Vest - 12 lbs"** is listed as a sample product in the dashboard — LegacyX may use this as a worked example product in the course.
