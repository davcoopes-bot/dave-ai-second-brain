## Summary

AMZScout's 9 mistakes that kill Amazon FBA businesses, summarised by Josh. Most are avoidable with upfront planning. Best used as a pre-launch checklist — knowing these failure modes before committing capital lets you design your approach around them.

## Key Takeaways

- **Mistake #1 — Wrong product**: Entering saturated markets, gut-feel validation. Fix: data before a dollar — target 20%+ margins and manageable competition.
- **Mistake #2 — Losing the Buy Box**: 80%+ of conversions go to Buy Box winner. Lost via pricing errors, bad metrics, stockouts, FBM fulfillment (FBA protects this).
- **Mistake #3 — Unoptimised listing**: Poor visibility and low conversion. Use Reverse ASIN Lookup for competitor keywords. Keyword stuffing makes titles unreadable and doesn't convert.
- **Mistake #4 — Bad PPC**: Targeting, negative keywords, ACOS monitoring are non-negotiable. Can't PPC your way out of a bad listing.
- **Mistake #5 — Ignoring policies**: Fake reviews, prohibited items, misrepresentation → account suspension or permanent ban. No reinstatement path for evasive behaviour.
- **Mistake #7 — Underestimating fees**: The full stack: referral (~15%) + FBA fulfillment + storage + returns + aged inventory. Run FBA Revenue Calculator on every product before sourcing.
- **Mistake #8 — Poor inventory management**: Stockouts kill Buy Box eligibility and organic ranking — sometimes permanently during launch. Watch IPI score. Overstocking eats cash in storage fees.
- **Mistake #9 — No branding**: Generic products = zero loyalty, no repeat buyers, no brand equity. Build from day one.
