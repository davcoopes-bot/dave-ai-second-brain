## Summary

YouTube video by Fields of Profit explaining the Online Arbitrage (OA) model for Amazon selling. OA = buy name-brand products cheap from retail stores → sell at the higher Amazon price. No product creation required. Dave and Josh are pursuing private label (PL), not OA — this source is filed as context for the alternative model and to make the OA vs. PL distinction explicit.

## Key Takeaways

- **OA is a different model to PL**: No brand ownership, no product creation, lower startup cost, faster time to first sale, lower margins, no brand equity building
- **OA loop**: Source (find price gap) → Validate (price consistency check) → Ship to FBA → Replenish winners
- **The real skill in OA**: Replenishing winners, not constantly finding new products
- **Professional Seller Account**: $40/month recommended over Individual — saves $1/unit, better Buy Box odds, easier ungating for big brands
- **Key OA tools**: SellerAmp (profit calculator + Keepa price history), Boxem (FBA shipping + analytics)

## Concepts Introduced or Reinforced

- Online Arbitrage (OA) vs. Private Label (PL) distinction — confirmed: PL is our model
- Reverse sourcing (find what successful sellers are selling, source the same items)
- Keepa (Amazon price history tracking)
