## Summary

Steve Chou's PPC framework for Amazon sellers, summarised by Josh. Built around one core rule: know your break-even ACOS before running a single ad. Uses a 3-tier campaign structure (Auto → Broad → Exact Match winners) with a specific workflow for graduating keywords between tiers based on conversion data. Clearest PPC framework in the batch — use this as the operational reference.

## Key Takeaways

- **Know your break-even ACOS first** — gross margin % is your ceiling. Spend above it and every sale loses money.
- **3-tier structure**: Auto (discovery) → Broad Match (phrase targeting) → Exact Match (proven winners at 25–50% higher bids)
- **Wait 1–2 weeks minimum** before touching campaigns; minimum 1,000 impressions per keyword before making any decisions
- **Listing conversion rate gate**: below 5% → fix the listing before touching ads; 5–10% acceptable; above 10% → pour fuel
- **During launch: intentionally run at/above break-even ACOS** — you're buying ranking, not profit
- **Target TACOS 8–15%** as organic ranking builds at scale
- **"Dynamic Bids — Down Only"** — do not use "Up and Down" (too unpredictable)
- Add all non-converters as **negative exact match keywords**

## Concepts Introduced or Reinforced

- ACOS (Ad spend ÷ ad revenue — advertising cost per sale as a %)
- TACOS (Total ad spend ÷ total revenue including organic)
- Break-even ACOS = gross margin %
- Negative keywords
- Dynamic bidding — down only
