## What This Source Is

Amazon's official product image requirements for Seller Central AU (G1881). Covers technical specs, main image rules, and requirements for all images. Referenced in the product detail page rules (G200390640) as the companion image compliance guide.

---

## Key Facts Extracted

- Every listing needs at least one compliant main image — no main image = listing suppressed from search
- Amazon recommends 6+ images plus one video
- Amazon may use images from multiple sellers and is not obligated to show yours
- Images can take up to 24 hours to appear after upload
