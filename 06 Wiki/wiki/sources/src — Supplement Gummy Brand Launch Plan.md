## Summary

Full launch plan for the confirmed FBA product direction: a premium supplement gummy brand. Three confirmed green lights (real H10 data): creatine gummy (253 avg reviews), magnesium glycinate gummy (994 avg reviews), NMN gummy (776 avg reviews). Same customer base across all three SKUs. Consumable = repeat purchase. Launch order: creatine first → magnesium → NMN. First sale target: September/October 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Gummy format is the entry point** — traditional powder/capsule versions of all three supplements are locked out by review moats. Gummy format is new enough that no brand dominates yet.
- **US GMP manufacturer required** — not standard Alibaba sourcing. Supplement manufacturing requires GMP certification + COA per batch + FDA compliance.
- **Brand matters more than in standard FBA** — supplement buyers respond to brand trust and packaging quality. Build premium, not generic.
- **Trademark early** — file USPTO Class 5 trademark and use Amazon IP Accelerator for faster Brand Registry access. Don't launch without Brand Registry.
- **Amazon Vine is essential** — $200/SKU for up to 30 early reviews. Most important launch lever for supplements.
- **Capital requirement: $8k–12k for creatine-only launch.** Full 3-SKU: $19k–35k. Dave has $60k liquid — plenty.
- **Creatine window is open but closing** — 253 avg reviews. Move fast.
