## RECITALS

## AGREEMENT

### 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Company** | DDJ Trading Pty Ltd, ABN 18 713 668 474 |

| **Directors** | Dave and Josh, each appointed as a director of the Company |
| **Shareholders** | Dave and Josh, each holding 50% of issued Shares |
| **Unanimous Resolution** | A decision requiring the agreement of both Shareholders |
| **Business** | The trading and commercial activities conducted by the Company |
| **Financial Year** | 1 July to 30 June of each year |

---

### 2. Share Structure

2.1 Upon execution of this Agreement, the issued share capital of the Company shall be held as follows:

| Shareholder | Shares Held | Percentage |
|-------------|-------------|------------|
| David Cooper | 50 | 50% |
| Joshua Foo | 50 | 50% |
| **Total** | **100** | **100%** |

2.2 All Shares carry equal voting rights, equal rights to dividends, and equal rights to surplus assets on winding up.

2.3 No new shares shall be issued without the **Unanimous Resolution** of both Shareholders.

---

### 3. Directors

3.1 Dave and Josh are each appointed as a Director of the Company from the date of this Agreement.

3.2 Each Director shall act in good faith and in the best interests of the Company, in accordance with their duties under the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth).

3.3 **Day-to-day operational decisions** may be made by either Director acting alone, within limits set by this Agreement.

3.4 **Major decisions** (listed in clause 5) require the agreement of both Directors.

3.5 Neither Director may be removed without the written consent of the other, unless removal is required by law.

---

### 4. Responsibilities & Roles

4.1 The Shareholders may agree in writing to assign specific operational roles. Absent such agreement, all duties and responsibilities are shared equally.

4.2 Each Shareholder agrees to:
- Devote reasonable time and effort to the Business
- Act honestly and in the best interests of the Company
- Not engage in any activity that competes directly with the Company without the other Shareholder's written consent
- Disclose any conflict of interest promptly

4.3 The allocation of roles and responsibilities may be reviewed and amended by Unanimous Resolution at any time.

---

### 5. Unanimous Decisions

The following matters require the written agreement of **both Shareholders** before the Company may proceed:

| # | Decision |

| 5.1 | Issuing new shares or changing the share structure |
| 5.2 | Amending the Company's constitution or this Agreement |
| 5.3 | Incurring any single debt or commitment exceeding **$5,000 AUD** |
| 5.4 | Entering into any loan, finance arrangement, or personal guarantee |
| 5.5 | Acquiring or disposing of any asset exceeding **$2,000 AUD** in value |
| 5.6 | Appointing or removing any employee, contractor, or director |
| 5.7 | Opening, closing, or changing signatories on any bank account |
| 5.8 | Distributing profits or declaring dividends |
| 5.9 | Winding up or dissolving the Company |
| 5.10 | Entering into any partnership, joint venture, or significant contract |
| 5.11 | Any material change to the nature or direction of the Business |

---

### 6. Banking & Finances

6.1 The Company shall maintain a dedicated business bank account in the name of the Company.

6.2 Both Directors shall be signatories on the Company bank account.

6.3 Transactions exceeding **$2,000 AUD** require the authorisation of both Directors.

6.4 Transactions below **$2,000 AUD** may be authorised by either Director acting alone, provided the expenditure is within the ordinary course of business.

6.5 Accurate financial records shall be maintained at all times. Each Shareholder has the right to inspect all financial records of the Company at any time with reasonable notice.

6.6 The Company shall lodge all required tax returns, BAS statements, and ASIC annual returns on time.

---

### 7. Profit Distribution

7.1 The Shareholders shall review the Company's financial position at the end of each Financial Year (and at any other time as agreed).

7.2 Profits available for distribution shall be split **50/50** between the Shareholders, unless otherwise agreed in writing by both parties.

7.3 Any reinvestment of profits into the Business must be agreed by Unanimous Resolution.

7.4 No Shareholder may draw funds from the Company without the knowledge and agreement of the other Shareholder, except for agreed salary or drawings as determined under clause 8.

---

### 8. Drawings & Director Fees

8.1 Neither Shareholder shall receive a salary, director's fee, or drawings from the Company unless agreed in writing by both Shareholders.

8.2 Where a salary or director's fee is agreed, it shall be set at the same rate for both Directors unless otherwise unanimously agreed.

8.3 Any agreed drawings or fees shall be documented and treated as a Company expense.

---

### 9. Capital Contributions

9.1 The initial capital contributions of each Shareholder are as follows:

| Shareholder | Initial Contribution |
|-------------|---------------------|
| David Cooper | $[Amount — to be agreed] |
| Joshua Foo | $[Amount — to be agreed] |

9.2 If the Company requires additional capital beyond initial contributions, both Shareholders shall be given the opportunity to contribute equally.

9.3 If one Shareholder contributes additional capital beyond the equal share, that contribution shall be treated as a **Director's Loan** to the Company, repayable on terms agreed in writing at the time of contribution, unless both parties agree to adjust equity.

9.4 No Shareholder may be required to contribute additional capital beyond their initial contribution without their written consent.

---

### 10. Transfer of Shares

10.1 Neither Shareholder may sell, transfer, assign, pledge, or otherwise dispose of any Shares without:
- First offering those Shares to the other Shareholder at fair market value (**right of first refusal**); and
- Obtaining the written consent of the other Shareholder.

10.2 If the non-transferring Shareholder does not wish to purchase the Shares at fair market value within **30 days** of written offer, the transferring Shareholder may offer the Shares to a third party, subject to the other Shareholder's written approval of the proposed transferee (not to be unreasonably withheld).

10.3 **Fair market value** shall be determined by agreement, or failing agreement, by an independent accountant appointed by mutual agreement (costs shared equally).

---

### 11. Death or Incapacity

11.1 In the event of the **death** or **permanent incapacity** of a Shareholder, the remaining Shareholder shall have the option to purchase the deceased/incapacitated Shareholder's Shares at fair market value within **90 days** of the event.

11.2 Fair market value shall be determined as per clause 10.3.

11.3 The Shareholders acknowledge the importance of holding appropriate life insurance and/or income protection insurance to fund any such buyout, and agree to review this position annually.

---

### 12. Exit & Dissolution

12.1 If both Shareholders agree to exit or wind up the Company, the process shall be:
1. All outstanding debts and liabilities of the Company shall be paid
2. Any remaining assets or cash shall be distributed **50/50**
3. The Company shall be deregistered in accordance with the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth)

12.2 If one Shareholder wishes to exit and the other wishes to continue the Business, the exiting Shareholder's Shares shall be offered to the remaining Shareholder under clause 10.

12.3 Neither Shareholder may unilaterally wind up the Company or take any action to deregister the Company without the written consent of the other.

---

### 13. Dispute Resolution

13.1 The Shareholders agree to resolve disputes in good faith and with a genuine effort to reach a mutually acceptable outcome.

13.2 If a dispute arises and cannot be resolved by direct negotiation within **14 days**, either Shareholder may require the dispute to be referred to **mediation** through a mutually agreed mediator (costs shared equally).

13.3 If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within **30 days** of referral, either party may pursue resolution through the courts of **Western Australia**, and both parties submit to the jurisdiction of those courts.

13.4 During any dispute, the Shareholders shall continue to perform their obligations under this Agreement and shall not take any unilateral action that could harm the Company.

---

### 14. Confidentiality

14.1 Each Shareholder agrees to keep confidential all proprietary business information, trade secrets, customer data, financial information, and strategies of the Company, both during and after their involvement with the Company.

14.2 This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of **2 years**.

---

### 15. Non-Compete

15.1 During their involvement with the Company, neither Shareholder shall engage in, or have a material interest in, any business that **directly competes** with the Company's Business without the prior written consent of the other Shareholder.

15.2 For **12 months** following a Shareholder's exit from the Company, they shall not solicit customers, suppliers, or employees of the Company for a competing business.

---

### 16. General

**16.1 Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Western Australia** and the *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth).

**16.2 Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, and agreements.

**16.3 Amendments:** This Agreement may only be amended in writing, signed by both Shareholders.

**16.4 Waiver:** Failure by either party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that provision.

**16.5 Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

**16.6 Counterparts:** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts (including electronically), each of which shall be deemed an original.
