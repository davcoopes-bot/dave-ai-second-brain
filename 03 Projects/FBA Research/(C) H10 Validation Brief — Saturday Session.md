## Pass/Fail Thresholds

Use the scoring framework. Here's the quick-reference version for Saturday:

### Revenue Potential (25 pts)
| Real data shows | Score |

| Most under $5k/month | 5–10 — market too small |

### Competition Gap (25 pts) — THE CRITICAL ONE
| Avg top-10 reviews | Score | What it means |
|-------------------|-------|---------------|
| < 200 | 20–25 | Easy entry. 3–4 months to compete. |
| 200–500 | 15–20 | Manageable. 6–9 months. Enter on sub-keywords. |
| 500–800 | 10–15 | Hard. Long-tail strategy + real PPC budget. |

## ⚠️ Contradiction Flag — Splatter Screen Main Image

The niche page currently recommends a **lifestyle shot as the main image** ("NOT a white-background product shot"). 

This is **wrong** per Amazon's image rules (G1881, ingested 2026-05-06):
- Lifestyle images as main images are only allowed for a limited list of product categories
- For most kitchen/cooking products, the main image **must** be a pure white background hero shot
- The lifestyle shots go in positions 2–6, not position 1

**Action:** The silicone splatter screen differentiation through imagery still stands — use lifestyle shots in secondary images. The main image must be white background with product at 85% fill. This needs to be corrected in the niche page.
