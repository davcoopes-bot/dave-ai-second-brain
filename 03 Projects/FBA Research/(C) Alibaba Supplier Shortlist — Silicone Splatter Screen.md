## Product Non-Negotiables

Every supplier must be able to deliver these before they make the shortlist. If they can't confirm all five, move on.

| # | Non-negotiable | Why |
|---|---------------|-----|
| 1 | **Full silicone wrap on handle** — not a rubber grip ring, not a silicone band. Full wrap. | Complaint #1 in competitor reviews is handle conducts heat. This is the headline claim. |
| 2 | **Coarser/open-weave mesh** — must clean under running water without scrubbing. Dishwasher-safe. | Complaint #2 is mesh clogs with grease. The fix must be built into the product. |
| 3 | **One-piece solid construction — no folding hinge** | Hinge failure is complaint #4. Foldable/collapsible versions exist — avoid them. |
| 4 | **Accurate sizing** — "fits 12-inch pan" must be based on actual pan measurement, not screen diameter | Mislabelled sizing is complaint #3. Confirm this before ordering samples. |
| 5 | **OEM/private label capable** — custom logo + packaging | Voya needs to be on the product, not a generic white-label |

## Supplier Evaluation Criteria

When reviewing each supplier profile on Alibaba, score them on:

| Criterion | What to look for |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Trade Assurance** | Must have it — Alibaba's protection if they don't deliver. Non-negotiable. |
| **Verified Supplier badge** | Prefer verified. Not a dealbreaker alone but adds confidence. |
| **Years on Alibaba** | 3+ years preferred. Less than 1 year = higher risk. |
| **Response rate** | 90%+ preferred. Low response rate = communication problems later. |
| **OEM/ODM capability** | Explicitly stated in their profile or product listing |
| **Sample availability** | Must offer samples. Confirm sample cost upfront (most charge $30–80 USD + freight). |
| **MOQ** | Target under 300 units for first order. 200 is ideal. Some will negotiate down. |
| **COGS range** | Target $5.00–$7.60 per unit landed. Anything above $8 pre-freight kills the margin. |
| **Lead time** | Standard production: 15–30 days. Ask specifically. |
| **Certifications** | Food-grade silicone (LFGB or FDA) — required for kitchen products on Amazon |

## Red Flags — Walk Away If You See These

- No Trade Assurance
- Trading company (not a manufacturer) — margins get eaten
- Can't do OEM/custom logo
- Foldable/collapsible only — won't do solid one-piece
- COGS above $8.00 pre-freight (kills margin at $29.99 sell price)
- No food-grade certification (LFGB or FDA) — Amazon kitchen products need this
- Response time > 48 hours on initial inquiry — bad sign for ongoing communication
