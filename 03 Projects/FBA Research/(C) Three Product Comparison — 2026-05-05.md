### Verdict
**🟡 Investigate More**

Great margin and strong demand, but the review ceiling is the problem. The top of the market (Iron Bull, Gymreapers) is well-dug-in with 1,600–2,800+ reviews. Entry strategy would need to target long-tail keywords and a real brand identity — not another generic wrap. Viable but not the easiest first product.

### The Opportunity
Common complaints: velcro wears out after 3–4 months, wraps too stiff on first use, inconsistent sizing. Fix: better velcro, pre-softened material, cleaner size guide. Listing gap: most competitors use white-background only — lifestyle gym imagery and benefit-led bullets would stand out. Dave's personal gym knowledge is a genuine brand advantage here.

### Next Steps
1. Pull real Helium 10 data on "powerlifting wrist wraps" and "wrist wraps women" long-tail keywords
2. Source 3 leading competitors from Amazon to benchmark quality
3. Revisit as a second product once a lower-competition product is established

### Verdict
**🟡 Investigate More**

Decent opportunity but risks are real — COGS sensitivity, price pressure from cheap generics, and the top two sellers are well-established. Differentiation is almost entirely in listing and branding, not the product. Viable only with a premium bundle play (more sizes, better packaging, a brand story) to avoid the race to the bottom.

### The Opportunity
Common complaints: lids don't seal odd-shaped bowls, sizes don't match advertised specs, some crack in dishwasher. Fix: better size range, dishwasher-safe certification, clearer size guide graphic. Listing gap: nobody shows the lids sealing actual bowls with food inside — that single lifestyle image would dramatically improve CTR.

### Next Steps
1. Pull real H10 data and verify COGS with 3–5 Alibaba suppliers
2. Test bundle angle (6pc + 9pc + storage pouch) to justify premium pricing
3. Evaluate only after Magnetic Spice Rack is assessed on real data

## FBA Research Report — Magnetic Fridge Spice Rack

**Date:** 2026-05-05
**Researched by:** Claude + Dave
**Data:** Simulated

### Product Overview
A magnetic spice rack that mounts directly to the side of a fridge, freeing up counter and cabinet space. Target customer is home cooks and anyone doing a kitchen organisation reset — a growing trend driven by social content (clean kitchen aesthetics). No dominant brand in the category. Most competitors are direct Alibaba-to-Amazon flips with no branding.

### Scores
| Category | Score | /Max | Notes |

| Revenue Potential | 18 | /25 | 3+ sellers at $5–10k/month, growing |
| Competition Gap | 18 | /25 | Lowest top-10 avg review count of all 3 products |

| Business Viability | 10 | /15 | 38.7% and $14.33 net per unit |
| **TOTAL** | **81** | **/100** | |

### Verdict
**✅ Green Light**

This is the clearest opportunity of the session. Lowest review bar to compete, most evenly distributed revenue across sellers, growing category, and the weakest competitor branding of any product researched today. Page 1 looks like Alibaba direct-to-Amazon with zero brand story — a well-executed brand (clean kitchen aesthetic, N52 magnets as the USP, premium packaging) has a realistic shot at a top-10 spot within 4–6 months. The $14.33 net margin per unit also means real cash flow once volume builds.

### The Opportunity
Common complaints: magnets not strong enough for heavier jars, racks scratching fridge surface, jar lids difficult to remove when rack is full, rust. Fix: N52 magnets, rubber-coated fridge backing, wider-mouth jars, matte stainless finish. Listing gap: nobody shows the product on an actual fridge in a clean, lifestyle kitchen — that image alone would drive CTR above the current page 1.

### Next Steps
1. Source 5–8 suppliers on Alibaba — benchmark N52 magnet strength, rust resistance, and jar quality
2. Order samples from top 3 suppliers (budget ~$150–200 AUD)
3. Brief a designer on brand identity: clean, minimal kitchen aesthetic
4. Run the fba-listing-optimization skill once samples are confirmed
