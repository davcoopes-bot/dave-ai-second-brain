# Supplement Gummy Brand — Launch Plan

> **Decision:** Build a premium supplement gummy brand. Three confirmed green lights in the same category (creatine 253 reviews, NMN 776 reviews, magnesium glycinate 994 reviews). Same customer, repeat purchase, scalable to 10+ SKUs.
> **Launch order:** Creatine first → Magnesium glycinate → NMN
> **Target first sale:** September/October 2026

---

## The Opportunity in One Paragraph

Supplement gummies work as an FBA play specifically when the gummy format is *new* relative to the supplement category. Creatine, magnesium glycinate, and NMN are all traditionally taken as powder or capsule. The gummy format for these specific supplements is recent enough that no brand has built a review moat yet. Creatine gummy has 253 avg reviews across the top 10 — that window will not stay open. The customer is the same across all three SKUs (health-conscious, gym-adjacent or longevity-focused), so every happy customer is a cross-sell opportunity. These are consumables — repeat purchase is built in.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Week 1–2)

Get the business infrastructure right before spending anything else.

### Amazon Seller Account
- [ ] Create **Professional Seller Account** ($39.99/month) — required for private label from day one
- [ ] Complete **Seller Identity Verification (SIV)** — passport + proof of address
- [ ] Complete **W-8BEN form** — certifies foreign seller status, prevents Amazon withholding 30% of all earnings
- [ ] Link **Wise account** for USD payments — Amazon US won't pay to Australian accounts

### Wise Account (US Banking)
- [ ] Open Wise account at wise.com — free, gives you a real US routing number + account number
- [ ] Use these details when setting up Amazon Seller Central

### Business Structure
- **Recommended:** Australian PTY LTD (already have trade income — add FBA under the same entity or a new one)
- Talk to your accountant about whether to run FBA income through existing structure or new PTY
- **Not required:** US LLC — you can sell on Amazon US as an Australian company. The AU-US tax treaty means FBA income is taxed in Australia only, not the US (FBA warehousing doesn't create "permanent establishment")
- US return address: set up a US 3PL or freight forwarder for customer returns (~$30–50/month)

## Phase 1 — Brand Identity (Week 2–4)

### Brand Name
Pick a name now — everything else depends on it.

Rules:
- Clean, premium feel — not too literal ("GummyStack" = bad, something like "Formly" or "Valo" = better)
- Search USPTO trademark database before committing: [USPTO TESS](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) — don't pick a name that's taken in Class 5 (dietary supplements)
- Short, memorable, works across all 3 SKUs and future supplements

### Trademark Filing
- **File USPTO trademark** in Class 5 (dietary supplements) — $250–350 per class
- Full registration takes 8–12 months, but you can access Amazon Brand Registry faster via:
  - **Amazon IP Accelerator** — use an Amazon-approved law firm, get Brand Registry access while application is pending. Costs ~$500–2,000 in legal fees but worth it for the Brand Registry access (A+ Content, brand protection, Amazon Vine)
  - Alternative: file in Australia first (faster, cheaper), use that for Brand Registry bridge while US processes

### Logo + Visual Identity
- Commission a logo and brand colour palette — budget $200–500 (99designs, Dribbble, Upwork)
- You need this before packaging design — packaging designer needs the brand assets
- Keep it clean, premium, supplement-adjacent. Think AG1, Momentous, Thorne — not your local gym brand

## Phase 2 — Manufacturer (Week 3–8)

This is the most important and most time-consuming phase. **Start this in parallel with Phase 1.**

### Why Supplements are Different from Standard FBA Sourcing
You cannot just find a supplier on Alibaba and go. Supplement gummies require:
- **GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) certified** facility — this is the baseline, non-negotiable
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** for every production run — lab-tested proof of ingredient purity and potency
- Proper supplement labelling compliance (Supplement Facts panel, structure/function claims, disclaimers)
- FDA prior notice filing if importing from outside the US

### US vs. Chinese Manufacturer

| | US Manufacturer | Chinese Manufacturer |
|---|---|---|
| COGS | $3–8/bottle | $0.50–2/bottle |
| FDA compliance | Built in — they handle it | You manage it — prior notice required for each import |
| Quality control | Easier to verify | Need independent inspection (QIMA/V-Trust, ~$300) |
| MOQ | 1,000–5,000 units | 3,000–10,000 units |
| Lead time | 4–8 weeks | 8–14 weeks + shipping |
| Recommendation | **Start here** | Move here after brand is proven |

**For launch, use a US GMP manufacturer.** Higher COGS, but you eliminate FDA import risk, quality risk, and lead time blowout. Once the brand is established and you know what sells, you can explore Chinese manufacturing to improve margins.

### US GMP Gummy Manufacturers to Contact
Research and get quotes from all of these:
- **Makers Nutrition** — makersnutrition.com — popular Amazon PL choice, gummy specialist
- **NutraCap Labs** — nutracaplabs.com — low MOQ, works with small brands
- **Gummy Industries** — gummyindustries.com — gummy specialist
- **Vitamer Labs** — vitamerlabs.com — GMP certified, supplement focus
- **Captek Softgel** — captek.com — larger operation, premium

### What to Ask Each Manufacturer
1. Are you FDA-registered and GMP-certified? Can you provide documentation?
2. What is your MOQ for gummies (per SKU)?
3. What is the lead time from approved formula to shipped product?
4. Do you have existing formulations for creatine, magnesium glycinate, and NMN gummies?
5. What does a sample order cost and how long does it take?
6. Do you provide COA for each batch?
7. Do you do in-house label printing or does that come separately?
8. What is your COGS per unit at 500, 1,000, and 5,000 unit volumes?

### Formulation Targets
| Product | Target dose per serving | Notes |
|---------|------------------------|-------|
| Creatine gummy | 3,000–5,000mg creatine monohydrate | 3g is common for gummies (taste/texture limits higher doses) |
| Magnesium glycinate gummy | 200–400mg elemental magnesium | Glycinate form specifically — best bioavailability, less laxative effect than citrate |
| NMN gummy | 250–500mg NMN | NMN is expensive — work backwards from COGS target |

### Samples
- Order samples from your top 2–3 manufacturers for each SKU
- Budget $300–600 for samples
- Test: taste, texture, smell, packaging quality, label accuracy
- Get independent lab verification on at least 1 sample (LabDoor, Informed Sport) — confirms the COA is accurate

## Phase 3 — Unit Economics (Week 4–5)

Run these numbers before placing any production order. If they don't work on paper, don't order.

### Formula Per Unit
```
Selling price
- Amazon referral fee (15%)
- FBA fulfillment fee (~$3.50–4.50 for supplement bottle)
- Manufacturing cost (COGS)
- Inbound shipping to FBA (~$0.50–1.00)
= Gross margin before PPC

- PPC spend (budget 20–25% of revenue at launch, aim for <15% long-term)
= Net margin
```

### Target Numbers
| Product | Price | Referral | FBA Fee | Target COGS | Shipping | Gross | PPC (22%) | Net |
|---------|-------|----------|---------|-------------|----------|-------|-----------|-----|
| Creatine gummy | $26 | $3.90 | $4.00 | $4.50 | $0.75 | $12.85 | $5.72 | **$7.13 (27%)** |
| Magnesium gummy | $21 | $3.15 | $4.00 | $4.00 | $0.75 | $9.10 | $4.62 | **$4.48 (21%)** |
| NMN gummy | $47 | $7.05 | $4.50 | $8.00 | $0.75 | $26.70 | $10.34 | **$16.36 (35%)** |

> **Note:** Magnesium margin is thin at $21. Competitors sit at $21 avg — you may be able to push to $24–26 with better branding and A+ content. NMN is the highest-margin SKU. Validate COGS with actual manufacturer quotes before finalising.

## Phase 4 — Amazon Setup & Listing (Week 6–10)

### Supplement Category Approval (Ungating)
Amazon requires approval to sell in Dietary Supplements. You'll need:
- Invoices from your GMP manufacturer (showing product name, quantity, your business name)
- Proof of GMP certification from manufacturer
- Possibly: lab test reports (COA)
- Submit via Seller Central → Add Product → request approval

This is standard — don't let it block you, but apply early so approval doesn't delay launch.

### Brand Registry
- Apply once trademark is filed (or through IP Accelerator for faster access)
- Unlocks: A+ Content, Brand Analytics, Amazon Vine, Sponsored Brands ads
- Essential — don't launch without Brand Registry if you can avoid it

### Listing Build (Per SKU)
Each listing needs:
- **Title:** Keyword-rich, lead with main benefit, brand name, key spec (e.g., "BrandName Creatine Gummies — 5000mg Creatine Monohydrate per Serving, 60 Gummies, Unflavored, GMP Certified")
- **7 bullet points:** Lead with benefit, include keywords naturally, address objections
- **A+ Content:** Brand story + product comparison module — requires Brand Registry
- **Backend keywords:** Fill every character of search term fields — synonyms, alternate spellings, related terms
- See [[wiki/fba/fba-listing-optimisation.md]] for full listing build guide

### Photography
Budget $300–800. You need:
1. Main image — product on white background, 85%+ fill, no text (Amazon requirement)
2. Lifestyle images (2–3) — product in use, supplement context
3. Infographic images (2–3) — ingredient callouts, dose breakdown, brand differentiators
4. Comparison image — your product vs. competitors (via A+ Content)

See [[wiki/fba/fba-image-requirements.md]] for full spec requirements.

## Phase 5 — Launch (Week 10–14)

### Pre-Launch Checklist
- [ ] Inventory arrived at FBA warehouse (track via Seller Central)
- [ ] Listing live and indexed for target keywords
- [ ] Brand Registry active
- [ ] Amazon Vine enrolled ($200 fee — gets you up to 30 reviews from Vine reviewers)
- [ ] PPC campaigns built and ready to activate

### PPC Launch Structure
Follow the 3-tier structure from [[wiki/fba/fba-ppc-strategy.md]]:
1. **Auto campaign** — let Amazon find keywords for 2 weeks, harvest data
2. **Broad match campaign** — test the winners from auto
3. **Exact match campaign** — lock in proven converters, drive efficiency

Launch ACOS target: 50–70% (you're buying rank, not profit — for the first 4–6 weeks)
Steady-state ACOS target: <30%

**Day 1–7 PPC budget:** $30–50/day per SKU. Drop to $20–30/day once rank is established.

### Review Generation
- **Amazon Vine** — enrol immediately after listing is live ($200/SKU). Best legitimate review source.
- **"Request a Review" button** — use this in Seller Central for every order (can be automated)
- **Insert card** — include in packaging: "Scan to register your purchase" → email sequence → review request at day 14. Keep it compliant (don't offer incentives for reviews).

### Pricing at Launch
- Launch at 10–15% below market average to gain velocity and rank
- Creatine: launch at $22–23, raise to $26 at ~30 reviews
- Magnesium: launch at $18–19, raise to $21 at ~30 reviews
- NMN: launch at $40–42, raise to $47 at ~30 reviews

## Phase 6 — Scale (Month 4+)

Once creatine is ranked, generating organic sales, and sitting at 4.2+ stars:
1. **Add magnesium glycinate SKU** — same launch sequence
2. **Add NMN SKU** — premium tier, cross-promote to existing customers
3. **Email list / brand store** — build direct customer relationship outside Amazon
4. **Subscription & Save** — enrol all SKUs. Supplement buyers subscribe. This is recurring revenue.
5. **Expand keyword attack** — as each SKU gains reviews, go after broader supplement keywords
6. **Future SKUs** — every supplement that's traditionally powder/capsule and hasn't gone gummy is a candidate. Keep the scraper running.

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Manufacturing lead time blows out | Medium | Start manufacturer outreach immediately — this is the longest lead time item |
| Amazon supplement category gating delays | Low–Medium | Apply early with proper documentation, have manufacturer provide GMP certs upfront |
| Creatine gummy reviews fill up before you rank | Medium | 253 reviews now — window is open but closing. Move fast on creatine. |
| Gummies taste bad → returns spike | Low if US manufacturer | Order samples from multiple manufacturers, test properly before committing |
| PPC costs exceed budget | Medium | Set daily caps, monitor weekly, stop campaigns that aren't converting |
| FDA compliance issue on label claims | Low if careful | No disease claims. Structure/function claims only ("supports muscle recovery" ✅, "treats muscle damage" ❌). Manufacturer should know this. |
| Amazon suspends listing (ingredient flagged) | Low | Creatine and magnesium glycinate are well-established. NMN has had some regulatory questions — research current FDA status before launch. |
