3. **Individual stock rule — the catalyst is the exit trigger, not the price:** ETF strategy is sound and stays on autopilot. For any individual stock position, the exit condition must be defined before buying — not after. The question is never "will it come back to my entry price?" It's "does the original thesis still hold?" If the thesis is invalidated (wrong results, bad announcement, fundamentals changed), sell regardless of the loss. If a catalyst is pending (flow test, earnings, announcement), hold until the result lands then decide immediately. Holding on hope is not a strategy. ERA and EXR are the case studies — ERA was a near-total loss held too long, EXR's Mongolian thesis died on announcement but a new QLD thesis emerged that warrants holding. Know the difference.
