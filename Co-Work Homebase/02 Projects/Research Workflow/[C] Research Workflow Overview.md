## Goal
Build a lightweight process for evaluating investment and business opportunities (stocks, crypto, FBA products, etc.) so I can start confidently, stay focused, and always end with a clear decision.

## Why
Research feels foggy — I don't know where to start, I get sidetracked, and I forget to save anything. Progress disappears and I end up back at square one.

## Tangible Outcomes
- A clear starting point for any analysis — no more staring at a blank page
- A consistent framework for evaluating opportunities across stocks, crypto, and FBA
- A way to save findings so progress carries over between sessions
- Every session ends with a verdict: buy / pass / watch / not yet

## Open Problems
1. ✓ No clear starting point — solved with the Research Kickoff Template
2. ✓ Losing clear vision on next steps after choosing an asset — solved with Post-Decision Checklists
3. ✓ Not saving findings — solved with Inbox auto-sort + Watchlist/Active/Archive folder structure
4. ✓ Turning analysis into a concrete decision — solved with the Verdict section in the Kickoff Template

## Files
- `[C] Research Kickoff Template.md` — fill this in before every research session
- `[C] Post-Decision Checklists.md` — run the right checklist after a Buy/Go verdict
- `[C] Research Index.md` — bird's-eye view of everything in Watchlist and Active
- `[C] Idea Generation Guide.md` — how to ask Claude for stock, crypto, and FBA ideas
- `Inbox/` — drop files here, auto-sorts every 30 seconds (requires launchd setup)
- `Watchlist/` — Stocks, Crypto, FBA subfolders for things being evaluated
- `Active/` — things you've committed to
- `Archive/` — closed out or passed on
