# Idea Generation Guide

*How to use Claude to find things worth researching.*

---

## How to ask for ideas

Just tell Claude what you're in the mood to look at. Be as loose or specific as you want — Claude will narrow it down and give you a shortlist worth evaluating.

### Stocks
> "Give me 5 stocks worth looking at right now — I'm interested in [tech / mining / healthcare / etc.]"
> "What sectors are showing momentum at the moment?"
> "I've got $X to deploy — what's worth researching?"

### Crypto
> "What are some undervalued crypto projects worth researching?"
> "Give me 3 altcoins with strong fundamentals to look at"
> "BTC is at [price] — is now a good time to look at adding?"

### FBA
> "Help me find a product gap on Amazon AU in the [kitchen / outdoor / pet] category"
> "I want a product under $30 selling price, low competition, evergreen demand — give me 5 ideas"
> "What are some trending product categories right now for FBA?"

---

## What Claude will give you

For each idea, Claude will give you:
- What it is and why it's worth looking at
- A quick risk flag (what could go wrong)
- A verdict on whether it's worth researching further

From there, pick the ones that interest you, create a file for each using the Research Kickoff Template, and drop them in the Inbox.

---

## Tips

- **Be specific about your constraints** — budget, risk tolerance, time horizon, market (AU vs US). The more specific you are, the better the ideas.
- **Don't research everything** — aim for 2–3 ideas at a time max. Depth beats breadth.
- **Use Claude during research too** — once you've started a Kickoff Template, paste it in and ask Claude to help fill in the gaps or challenge your thinking.
